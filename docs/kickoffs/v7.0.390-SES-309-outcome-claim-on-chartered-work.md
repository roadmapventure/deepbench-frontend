## 2. CONTEXT

**The instrument exists; nothing chartered can use it.** Measured this session on live Supabase:

- `public.ticket_outcome`'s `claim` CTE splits `backlog_items.enhancement_claim` on `:` into a metric and a direction. Its verdict CASE: `pending` when no scoreboard row exists ≥ 72h after the ship; else `unmeasurable` when the metric is NULL or the direction is not `up`/`down`, or when either side lacks the column; else `held` / `did_not_hold`.
- The column comment already says the claim is *"optional on any ticket; SES-303 grades it"* — so the design intent was never enhancement-only. But the only rule that requires it is `EL-01` (enhancement admission), the filing template in `session-setup.md` step 3b does not include it, and **zero of the 137 Selfbuild tickets carry one** (`enhancement_claim IS NULL` for every row; `scope_origin = 'enhancement'` count is 0). The two `ticket_outcome` rows (SES-303, SES-277) are `pending` and will resolve `unmeasurable` on 2026-09-05.
- The view cannot tell **"this ticket never declared a claim"** from **"this ticket declared one the scoreboard cannot measure"**. Both read `unmeasurable`. The first is a filing omission a reviewer should see; the second is an honest verdict. Conflating them is what makes the board silent.
- `drain_epic_next()` and `prime_directive_queue()` read `enhancement_claim` **only** inside the enhancement-lane admission test (`scope_origin = 'enhancement'`). A claim on a chartered ticket does not touch the pick path. Verified by reading both function bodies.

**What "honest" means here, decided by the design session (Tier 2, decide-and-flag):** a chartered ticket's claim names one of the seven `platform_scoreboard` numbers and a direction, **or** says `none: <why no scoreboard number applies>`. Most M5 work is the second kind — `SES-308` records confirmations in `runner_heal_signatures`, not on the scoreboard — and saying so on the row is the point: the board then shows a declared `unmeasurable`, never a silent one. The alternative in the ticket (a separate `outcome_claim` column) was rejected: the column comment already grants the wider meaning, the two pick functions only read it under the enhancement test, and a second column would be the one-fact-two-homes defect.

**Not this ticket:** new scoreboard metrics (the M6 gate's question), the FILE-MATRIX fail-loud tripwire (the `unclaimed` verdict on the board is the loud signal for now; extending the tripwire is a follow-up if John wants it), anything in `drain_epic_next()`.
