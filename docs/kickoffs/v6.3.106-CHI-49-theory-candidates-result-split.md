## CONTEXT

First of the `CHI-49`/`50`/`51` follow-up cluster (`design-chi-49-0721`, John, 2026-07-21) spun out of `design-evidence-drawers-0721`'s taxonomy conversation, captured as a decision record in `STYLE-GUIDE.md` §40's closing note. `CHI-46` (News as a 3rd persistent drawer) already shipped; this session is the first of the not-yet-built pieces that note flagged. `CHI-50` (Nadia's proposal becomes its own "Draft Forecast" drawer) and `CHI-51` (drawers survive resolution instead of being wiped; auto-open only mid-flow, not once resolved) are separate, larger follow-up kickoffs, not this one — `CHI-51` explicitly depends on this session landing first.

**The problem:** the single Theory `<Drawer>` conflates two different kinds of content — Priya's offered candidate theories (a choice the user makes) and the eventual test result (the narrative that comes back from acting on that choice). Every other Evidence drawer (News, Analysis) already holds exactly one kind of thing; Theory doesn't — John's "papers on a desk" principle.

**The fix, confirmed live with John (this session, restated and re-confirmed after a naming question):** split into 2 drawers along the flow's own stage boundary —
- **`{intentLabel} Candidates`** (e.g. "Theory Candidates", or "Forecast Candidates"/"Correct Candidates" when `hypFlow.intent` is classified differently — **intent-dynamic, matching how the single drawer's title works today**, not a hardcoded "Theory" string) — covers `generating`/`choosing`. Renders whenever `hypFlow` exists, so it never disappears once a pick is made; the moment a candidate is chosen (`CHI-44`'s auto-fire into `testing`), it collapses and its title gains a chosen-summary: `"Theory Candidates — 4 offered, chose: \"[text]\""`.
- **`{intentLabel} Result`** — covers `testing`/`result`/`committing`/confirmation. Only starts existing once a theory is actually chosen (nothing to show before that). Shows the "Priya is testing…" placeholder until the real result lands, then the exact same evidence content (tables/chart/verdict) as today, unchanged.

`computeAutoCurrent` priority becomes: `hyp-result` (once testing has started) > `hyp-candidates` (still choosing) > `qa` > `news`.

**Two implementation decisions made this session (Tier 1/2, not separately re-confirmed with John — cheap to revise, flagged here):**
1. The Candidates drawer, once collapsed, still shows real content if manually reopened: a read-only recap of every candidate offered, the chosen one marked "Chosen" with a brass border, no click handlers. Without this, reopening it after a pick would show an empty body — the "papers on a desk" persistence this whole cluster is about requires there to actually be something to read.
2. The existing "Submitted theory" block (the chosen text, shown today regardless of stage) moves into the Result drawer, not duplicated into Candidates — it's context for what's being tested, not part of the offering step. The Candidates drawer's own collapsed-title summary already names the chosen text at a glance.
