## 2. CONTEXT

**Measured live 2026-09-02, not recalled.** The M5 drain (`runner_directives` `238aa9ca`, 16 named members) has every `milestone_required = true` member `done` — the M5 gate record says *"M5 is complete when those close"* and it is complete — yet `drain_epic_next()` returns `blocked`, not `retired`, because its retirement predicate is

```sql
SELECT count(*) INTO v_open_now FROM runner_drain_scope s JOIN backlog_items b ON b.id = s.item_id
 WHERE s.directive_id = d.id AND b.status <> ALL (c_finished);
```

and four named members are not finished: `DAT-25`, `SES-123`, `SES-82` (all `defer_status = 'yes'`, ruled non-required at the M5 gate) and `SES-292` (open, non-required). `SES-305` made deferral pick-side only, on the principle that a pick-side clause must never leak to the retirement side. So the drain can never retire, step 8d's gate-review sweep (which reads `status = 'done'` drains) never fires, and directive `0970abad`'s succession never declares M6. **The next milestone cannot start on its own** — charter goal 1 fails at the handoff.

**This is not the forbidden edit, and the kickoff must say why in the runbook.** The three runbook passages forbid moving a *pick-side* clause (`delivered`, the flags, `blocked_by`, `defer_status`) to the retirement side, because each would retire a drain *on the runner's own say-so*. `milestone_required` is the opposite kind of fact: it is set only at a milestone's gate decision (`SES-304`, `M5-04`: *"set at its gate decision and never re-judged per question"*), by John or by the gate sitting under `M6-01` with a 72-hour reversal window. A drain whose required members are all finished retires on **the gate's word**, which is the authorisation the boundary exists to protect. Deferred and non-required members are not "not work" — they stay pickable under Prime Directive §2(c) after the drain retires — they are simply not the finish line.

**Fail closed for drains that predate the flag.** A drain whose named list carries no `milestone_required = true` row at all (M0–M4 shapes, or any future drain declared without a gate ruling) keeps today's all-members rule. The new rule engages only when the finish line has been ruled.

**A deferred REQUIRED member still holds the drain open.** Deferral of a required member (as `SES-82` was, before the M5 gate removed it from the set) is a signal the gate must re-rule, not a reason to retire. The census already reports it (`"N deferred (…)"`, `SES-305`).
