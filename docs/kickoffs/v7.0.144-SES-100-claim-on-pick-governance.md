## What the claim does and does not protect — the substance of the edit

This is the part worth getting right, because a half-understood coordination rule is worse than
none:

- **It serializes ticket selection.** One `UPDATE … WHERE claimed_by IS NULL OR claimed_at <
  now() - INTERVAL '24 hours' RETURNING` — the write *is* the reservation. 1 row = yours,
  0 rows = someone else holds it, take the next queued ticket.
- **It does NOT serialize the `dev` branch.** Two sessions still land on one branch; that is
  fetch → rebase → push, retried up to 3 times (register B42).
- **It does NOT serialize the briefing republish.** That is the publish lease — the repurposed
  `runner_lease` singleton at a 10-minute TTL over the serial tail, plus re-fetch-and-re-harvest
  before republishing.
- **The 24h expiry is why a dead session cannot strand a ticket.** A session that vanishes
  mid-build holds its claim for at most 24 hours; after that the ticket is re-claimable and the
  board heals without anyone intervening.
