**Root cause:** Alex never reformats the clean Data Room fact directly — he reformats *Priya's own analytical prose*, which correctly carries caveats/hedging for her own schema (that's appropriate there) but by the time it reaches Alex it (a) consumes nearly the entire 1500-token output budget just restating/summarizing the required `headline`/`supports`/`complicates`/`consider`/`key_data_points` fields, leaving no real room for the optional `visualization` object, and (b) reframes a clean current-vs-target number behind enough hedging that Alex's own (correctly conservative, per `ARCHITECTURE.md` §19g) chart-inclusion criterion reads it as unresolved/multi-factor more often than the underlying fact warrants.
