this incident for ever.** The only cycle in a position to report a silence is by construction alive.
At the real recovery (`2026-08-28T03:42:00Z`) the newest row was **zero minutes old**, so a
newest-row-age alarm reports *clear*. This ship's own live run prints both numbers side by side:
`currentGapHours: 0.16` beside `worst.hours: 38.97`.

**2. The ticket's own premise is a UTC-day claim wearing a calendar-day fix.** `SES-269` says
*"2026-08-27 produced ZERO rows"* — true of the **UTC** day. On **John's** clock (America/Chicago,
the boundary step 3 mandates for every "today", directive `1d01ea85`), **2026-08-27 held two rows**:
`03:42Z` and `04:42Z` UTC are 22:42 and 23:42 CST. So the obvious build — *"was there a day with no
rows?"* — **does not fire on the incident the ticket was filed from**, and no day-bucket form on any
clock can see a hole shorter than a day.

Both are negative controls in the guard, run on the same fixture and asserted to lose.
