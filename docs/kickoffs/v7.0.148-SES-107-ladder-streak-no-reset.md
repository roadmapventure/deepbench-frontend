## 2. CONTEXT

The written ladder rule is *"Accept → streak +1 (5 consecutive → rung +1)"* and it **does not say
what happens to the streak after a promotion.** Cycle `7392e345` hit that blank live at 20:27Z:
John's Accept took `tooling` from streak 4 to 5, which promoted it rung 6 → 7, and the cycle had
to choose. It set the streak to 0 and — correctly — filed `q-ladder-streak-reset` rather than
letting an invented rule stand silently.

**John answered NO at 2026-08-21T22:04Z** ("Yes = confirm reset to 0. No = it should carry over"),
and asked on the card, verbatim:

> *"which one just keeps the count going? no need to reset - why would i do that?"*

The reason a reset existed at all is a runaway. With the rule written as *promote at 5 **or
more***, a streak left at 5 promotes again on the very next Accept, and on the one after that —
a rung per tap, forever. That is not what John asked for either; it is the other failure. The
form that gives him exactly what he asked for **without** the runaway was put to him on the card
and not contradicted:

> **promote on every 5th Accept (5, 10, 15, …), never on "at least 5", and never reset the streak.**

Stated as a test rather than a sentence — `streak % 5 = 0` — the ambiguity cannot come back,
which is the whole point of the ticket.

**Where the rule lives.** Nowhere in code. `grep -rl "runner_ladder" --include=*.js` returns
nothing: the ladder is applied by hand, in SQL, by each cycle at harvest time. So the rule's only
home is these two runbooks, and a wording that can be read two ways *is* the defect.
