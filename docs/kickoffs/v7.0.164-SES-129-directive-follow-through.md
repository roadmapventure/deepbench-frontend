## The measurement that decided the design

The obvious implementation **derives** the outcome by joining `runner_directives.acted_cycle` →
`runner_cycles` and reading `item_id`. Read live this cycle, that `text` column holds **five
different shapes** across the 24 closed directives:

| Directive | `runner_cycles.item_id` |
|---|---|
| `dda69acb` | `ee6ac097-5834-48fa-a8ea-78b3f599af71` (a `runner_items` uuid) |
| `edab5908` | `directive edab5908` (prose) |
| `c4d95dc7` | `c4d95dc7-9df4-4de4-8d5e-8d4d95f7d2f2` (the directive's **own** id) |
| `34865f07` | `directive 34865f07 — .claude/ stall mechanism named; unattended prohibition restored on evidence` |
| `a55155f3` | `SES-86 phase 2 — materialized queue numbers (register B4)` |

Rendering that into John's card gives him a column of uuids and half-sentences — the same trap
`backlog_items.title` carries (`SES-91`). `item_ref` is no fallback: populated on **3 of 24**.
So the outcome is **stored**, not derived.

## The design — store what cannot be derived, derive what can

**Stored** (migration `ses129_directive_outcome`): `outcome` + `outcome_note` on
`runner_directives`. `outcome` is CHECK-constrained to
`shipped` / `carded` / `superseded` / `closed_unrecorded`; `outcome_note` is the sentence John
reads, in his register.

**Derived at render time, deliberately given no stored value:** a *live* directive's state.
`waiting`, `in progress`, a **standing** drain-epic and an **active** budget override all follow
from `type` + `status` + `expires_at`, which are already columns and cannot go stale. `standing`
is the one that matters: `SES-111` property (2) says a drain is **never consumed**, so it sits at
`status='queued'` forever *by design* — a card that rendered it as "waiting to be picked up" would
be telling John the opposite of the truth about the directive he is actually being served by.

**`close_directive()` is the structural half.** Step 9's *"mark the directive `done`"* becomes one
call that **cannot set the status without also recording the outcome**. The alternative is a second
write every cycle must remember, and this platform has paid six times over for that lesson
(`SES-86` phase 3, `v7.0.146`, `SES-101`, `SES-111`, `SES-127`, `SES-128`).

## Deliberately NOT done, stated here rather than left to be discovered

- **The 24 historical directives are backfilled to `closed_unrecorded`, uniformly, and their real
  outcomes are NOT reconstructed.** Three of them sit next to a real shipped SHA and their
  `outcome` could be inferred — but the **note** is the half John reads, and there is no stored
  wording to recover, only wording a migration would invent. Same call `SES-128` made for the eight
  unslotted readings, for the same reason. Stamping the value uniformly is also what lets `NULL`
  mean **defect** from here on rather than "old row".
- **The page cannot know when John *typed* a directive**, only when a cycle **recorded** it.
  `briefing-state`'s `directive` is a bare string with no timestamp, where `reading` carries an
  `at`. `runner_directives.created_at` is therefore the *harvest* time and can lag his typing by up
  to a cycle's cadence (~3h). The line is worded **"recorded"**, not "saved", rather than shipping a
  timestamp that is confidently wrong. The fix — give `directive` an `at` in `briefing-state`, the
  same shape `reading` already has — is named on this ticket's card for a later ticket.
