## 2. CONTEXT

John opened the session with `SES-101` — "New automation tickets file themselves into the
automation lane" (Tooling · `P10 - Tooling`) — because its ship card was on his briefing asking for
his Accept. The ticket closed `done` at `v7.0.203`, and he had already accepted its first ship card
on 2026-08-21. The card is a dead question.

**Measured live before a line changed, and it is not one card.** Of the 10 undecided
non-gated cards, **7** sit on tickets already `done` (`SES-101` `9cdf840b`, `SES-121` `8c421bf3`,
`SES-140` `bfd7598b`, `SES-146` `b1ca9305`, `SES-147` `6ce64ed2`, `SES-149` `9a5e922b`, `SES-162`
`c1af750f`). The 3 live rows: `SES-154` `5c220f71` and `SES-157` `1f68482a` (both tickets
`delivered` — real asks), and `edb78e0c` (no `backlog_id` — always renders).

**Why the rule says what it says today, and why that reason is stale.** `briefing_open_cards()`
(`v7.0.199`, directive `16b3ff73`) retires only `gated_before_build` cards, by deliberate design:
a gated card asks *permission* (moot once built); a ship card asks for a *rating*, the trust
ladder's only input, "meaningful forever." **That rationale predates `SES-154` (`v7.0.205`) by one
day.** Since `SES-154`, a ship writes the ticket `delivered` and **only John's Accept writes
`done`** — so a rating-in-waiting now sits on a `delivered` ticket, never a `done` one. A ship card
on a `done` ticket means the verdict already happened (or, for the 7 transition stragglers, a
pre-`SES-154` cycle closed it under the old rule). Retiring it starves nothing.

**John's approval, 2026-08-23, with the trade stated:** proceed; the 7 straggler ships leave his
page unrated and never feed the trust ladder; the retired-strip copy on the page generalizes.

## 6. DESIGN RULES

- **Terminal, not decided-elsewhere:** the predicate keys on `('done','removed')` only.
  `delivered`, `open`, `partial`, `removal proposed` all render. This is what keeps `SES-154`'s
  acceptance loop intact — the card IS the Accept mechanism for a `delivered` ticket.
- **Label, never hide** — `render` filters, `retired_reason` explains, the strip reports the count.
- **Never decide on his behalf** — `decision` stays NULL on retired rows (§19v).
- **Derived, not maintained** — a ticket reaching `done` drops its dead cards with no write.
