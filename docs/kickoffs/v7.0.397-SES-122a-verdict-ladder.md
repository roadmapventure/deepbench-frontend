1. **A verdict is a ladder signal.** `approve` → `promote` (streak +1, rung +1 every 5th, `SES-107`); `block` → `reset` (streak 0, rung unchanged — a red gate is the verifier's judgment on one ship, not John's reversal of a delivery, so it costs the streak but not the rung). 2. **`reset` is a third signal on `ladder_apply_signal`** (same signature — `CREATE OR REPLACE` is safe; assert one overload).
3. **One function answers what a rung buys:** `class_autonomy(p_priority_class)` — `auto_done` when `rung ≥ runner_settings.auto_done_rung` (seeded **3**); `extra_files`/`extra_tasks` = `greatest(0, rung − runner_settings.cap_relax_rung)` (seeded **5**). Columns, not literals. Parts (b) and (c) are its consumers.
4. **`apply_ladder_decision()` keeps its gated short-circuit and its tap semantics for the historical rows**, but its inline digit CASE is replaced by a call to `ladder_work_class()` — one home (`SES-286a` named this duplicate as the thing this ticket removes).
