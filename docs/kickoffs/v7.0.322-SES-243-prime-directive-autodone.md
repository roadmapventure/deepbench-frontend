### The three decisions this ticket left to its build cycle

**(a) A LOOKUP, NOT A CONSTANT.** §2f's own closing sentence is *"at Selfbuild completion or
revocation, §2f lapses and SES-154 resumes in full."* A boolean constant would have to be un-set by
hand and would outlive the word that authorised it — the exact class of rule this platform has
watched go silently unfollowed eight times. Reading the directive row means the widening lapses on
its own terms, with nothing for a cycle to remember. This is the same reasoning the file already
applies to epic and priority class ("WHY THE ELIGIBILITY TEST READS THE BOARD AND NOT THE ARGV").

**(b) KEYED ON THE BODY PREFIX, NOT THE UUID — and the trade is stated in both directions.** A uuid
key cannot false-positive, but John has already re-declared this directive once
(`071fc16a` → `a0ef9525`); under a uuid key a re-declaration would silently lapse the widening he
had just restated. The body prefix tracks re-declarations. Its cost is a false-positive risk, paid
for by **anchoring the match at the start of the body**: the directive opens with the phrase, while
rows that merely discuss it — `SES-243`'s own ticket text among them — quote it mid-body.

**HONEST BOUND, measured rather than claimed:** on today's ledger the anchored and unanchored forms
return the **same single row** (`a0ef9525`), so the anchor is not catching a live false positive —
it guards a future one. Said plainly rather than presented as a caught defect.

**(c) THE DEFAULT IS THE SAFETY.** `undefined`, absent, `null`, `false` and a failed lookup are
**one answer** — *not proven live* — and all keep the narrow `P10 - Tooling` rule. Unknown costs
John one tap; the other direction costs him a `done` he never authorised. This is also what keeps
`tests/regression/SES-181-verifier.js` passing **byte-unchanged**: its `ELIGIBLE` fixture carries no
such key, so it now doubles as the "directive absent → charter rule" control.

### What was deliberately NOT built, named rather than left to be found

§2f has a second half: where the verifier *structurally cannot grade* a ship (a diff in
`deepbench-backups-offsite`, or a self-certifying-path edit) the ship auto-dones on recorded
evidence plus a green CI run. **That path is not in this file and must not be.** Implementing it
here would have the verifier bless precisely the edits charter premise 3 bars it from grading, and
`SELF_CERTIFYING_PATHS` would be the invariant laundered. It is a cycle-side judgement about
evidence, not a verdict. Carried as this ticket's declared remainder.
