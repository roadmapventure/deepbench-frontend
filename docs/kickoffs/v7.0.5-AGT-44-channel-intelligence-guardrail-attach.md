### What was missed, and how it was found

The v6.3.229 close-out claimed the Skill reached "every artifact-producing agent." It reached 2 of
3. `design-arch-beta-0729`'s pre-regression round 3 failed case 24 (`news-first-card`) on
`platform_language_detected` — the one criterion this guardrail exists to satisfy — with every
other criterion passing on Marcus's `answer` artifact. Checked live in `capability_skill_profiles`:
no row exists for `capability_slug: 'channel-intelligence'`. Verified again this session,
unchanged: still no row.

### Why `quality-gate` and `project-manager` are deliberate exclusions, not gaps

Both were candidates in the original design and both were turned down, for reasons that still
hold:
- **`quality-gate` (Owen Marsh — The Proofreader):** attachment is capability-wide with no
  intent-level scoping. Owen's judging intents must name criteria/field names
  (`confidence_tier`, artifact type, etc.) to function — a guardrail that bans naming platform
  mechanics would degrade his own judging vocabulary. His separate defect (rejection text leaking
  jargon to the *user*, not to his own judging prompt) is `AGT-52`, filed and open — not fixed by
  attaching this Skill to his capability.
- **`project-manager` (Michelle Manning — Project Manager):** her routing reasoning renders in the
  Agent Routing drawer, where the mechanics ARE the content a user is meant to see (hop names,
  delegation targets). A guardrail banning that vocabulary would break the drawer's own purpose.

Verified live this session: neither capability carries the attachment today (confirmed by the
coverage query in Task 2) — the exclusions are still accurately "never attached," not "attached
then silently removed."
