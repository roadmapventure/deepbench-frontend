## What was measured, and the two corrections the measurement forced

Mechanical census delegated to a **Sonnet 5** subagent (register B21 — `git log`/`grep`
gathering is the mechanical shape); the judgment — scope, risk, and what the numbers mean — stayed
on the Opus 5 orchestrator. Full record: `docs/harvests/SES-121.md`.

**Correction 1 — a per-file commit table cannot answer "how much does this directory churn", and
the first draft summed one.** Commits touch several files at once, so the column double-counts,
and the raw directory total counts files that no longer exist. Re-measured directly with
`:(exclude)` pathspecs:

| Window | `.claude/` | less `inflight/` | of which the 3 `SKILL.md` candidates | residue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All time | 78 | **13** | 8 | 5 |
| Last 14 d | 39 | **10** | **7** | **3** |

**65 of 78 all-time `.claude/` commits (83%) were `.claude/inflight/*.md`** — and register B41
already moved those out (last such commit 2026-08-21). The precedent the ticket cites removed
five-sixths of the problem before this ticket was picked.

**Correction 2 — the inbound-reference grep matched full paths only, and `CLAUDE.md`'s own
pointer table uses the directory form.** Re-measured on the directory form and excluding
historical records (kickoffs, `SESSIONS.md`, `FEATURES-ARCHIVE.md`, `harvests/`, the snapshot):
`session-setup` **7** live referrers, `session-hygiene` **1**, `triage` **1** — all outside
`.claude/`, so a cloud cycle can repoint every one. And `.claude/rules/` **28**, of which
**23 are live source files** in `src/`/`api/`/`lib/`/`scripts/` carrying `**Read first:**`
header comments.

## The proposal filed for John

Extract the **body** of the three churning skills to `docs/runbooks/session-setup.md`,
`docs/runbooks/session-hygiene.md`, `docs/runbooks/triage.md`, leaving each `SKILL.md` as
frontmatter plus a one-line pointer, and repoint the nine live referrers in the same commit.
Explicitly **not** in scope: `.claude/rules/*` (23 live source files name it; 12 of 14 files have
a single creation commit), `discovery`/`reframe` (1 commit each), and the two config files.

**The honest caveat, on the card as well as here:** this does not unblock the four items already
waiting and cannot — performing the extraction is itself a `.claude/` write. It needs one session
John attends, and the right ask is that the same sitting lands the carded one-liners too.
