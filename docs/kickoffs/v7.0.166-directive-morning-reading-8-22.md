## The two halves that would have shipped wrong

**(a) A DB-only fix passes QA and leaves the card blank.** The slot lives in **two homes**: the
ledger column, and `briefing-state.reading` — which is what `readingSlot()` actually reads
(template:432–433; it never queries Supabase). Update only the row and every SQL assertion goes
green while John's Morning row stays empty — QA passing while the one thing he asked for
("**shown on the card**") did not happen. Both homes move, and the QA asserts on the **served
page**.

**(b) Claiming this turns calibration on.** It does not, and saying so would be the "confident
wrong number" `SES-128`'s four guards exist to prevent:

- `derive_token_allowance()` needs a **night→morning** pair. There is still **no night reading**,
  so it returns `guard = 'no bracketing pair: no night reading'` — asserted *after* the move.
- Tonight's night reading will **not** pair with this one either. The function takes the latest
  `night`, then the earliest `morning` **after** it (`WHERE slot='morning' AND taken_at >
  v_night.taken_at`), so the bracket runs forward. 8/22's morning sits *before* tonight's night.
- Today's allowance is therefore unchanged: it is John's unexpired `budget_override` `43a9d4ae`
  (`max_tokens` 25,000,000, expires `2026-08-23T05:00Z`), precedence rule (1) in step 3.

**What John gets** is his reading on the card where he put it, his declaration on the ledger, and
the correctly-labelled half of a future bracket. **What turns calibration on** is a Night reading
tonight and a Morning reading tomorrow within 24 hours of it.
