## CONTEXT

Fifth of the (now larger) `CHI-42`–`CHI-5x` cluster (`design-evidence-drawers-0721`, John, 2026-07-21). `CHI-42`/`43`/`44`/`45` already shipped (v6.3.90/93/97/100). This is the original, narrowest-scoped piece of a since-expanded conversation (Theory splitting into Candidates/Result, Nadia's proposal becoming its own "Draft Forecast" drawer, and cross-flow persistence are separate, larger follow-up kickoffs — not this one).

**The problem:** News cards currently only exist inside `EvidenceColumn`'s true-empty-state early return (`if (!qaEvidence && !hypFlow) { ... }`, L1720–1775) — the *only* place they render. The instant you click a headline, `analyzeNewsCard()` calls `submit()`, which sets `qaEvidence`, which makes that whole branch (including the other 2 unclicked headlines) stop rendering forever. There's no way back to them without a reload or Clear.

**The fix, confirmed live with John:** News becomes a real 3rd drawer, titled **"News"**, participating in the exact same `computeAutoCurrent`/`isDrawerOpen`/manual-override/auto-scroll mechanism `CHI-43` already built for Analysis/Theory — extended from 2 keys to 3. Priority order (confirmed): Theory wins if active, else Analysis, else News (News is the natural default when nothing else is happening — it's what's on screen at page load). Once something else becomes current, News collapses to its header but never disappears — same drawer, same 3 original headlines, one click away.
