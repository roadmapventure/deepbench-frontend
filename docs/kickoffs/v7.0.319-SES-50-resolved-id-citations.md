## The design choice this cycle owned

The ticket says *"correct the `§19i` sentence"*. The obvious execution — delete it — is wrong, and
that is the whole of the design here. **The sentence welds two facts together:**

- a **dead citation** (`LOG-28`'s question, answered), and
- a **live requirement** (*"All current entries must be re-verified against real sources … before
  the model is considered complete"*), which `AI-35` still carries — read live from
  `public.backlog_items` at this ship: **`status = 'partial'`**.

So the fix **records the resolution and keeps the open half explicit**, saying in the same breath
that the audit is unfinished. A deletion would have passed any "the stale phrase is gone" check
while silently retiring a requirement nobody voted to retire. Clause `the-live-requirement-survives`
in the guard exists precisely to fail that build.

**`[LOCKED]` was not treated as a bar.** Locked governs the *decision*; the decision here is
untouched. What changed is the *currency of a citation* — and a locked section is the worst place
for a stale one, because readers trust it most. The ticket's own instruction (*"do not re-litigate
any locked decision"*) is honoured: no decision moved.
