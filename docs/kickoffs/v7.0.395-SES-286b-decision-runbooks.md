**What is a decision, decided here (M6-01):** any write a cycle or session makes **on judgment rather than on a rule's mechanical output** — resolving `needs-decision`, deferring (`defer_status = 'yes'`/`'stuck'`), removing under `M6-03`, re-tiering or re-homing a ticket, amending a required set, ruling a gate, amending a directive. **Not a decision:** a ship's own close-out status write on a green verdict (that is the verifier's output, and its Reverse lives on the ship card), a queue recompute, a claim, a before-image, a scoreboard stamp. A decision that files a ticket files it in the same transaction (`M6-05`).
