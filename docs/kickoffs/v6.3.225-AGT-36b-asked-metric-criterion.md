## 2. CONTEXT

**`S-AGT-36` shipped and its scorer works. This patches one criterion that was measured wrong.**

That session's live run: both honest-gap cases FAILed, both on `quantitative_content_present`. Root cause is not the implementation — it is the criterion. **`quantitative_content_present` asks whether a number is present, when the thing worth catching is whether the agent reported a figure for the quantity it just said it could not answer.**

Measured, case 23 (`south-korea-coop`), Owen — Proofreader's real verdict:
- `platform_language_detected` **false**, `actionable_guidance_present` **true**, routing to *"your regional channel manager or the partner operations team covering that geography"* — **both of the bars that were actually broken are now fixed**, versus the recorded pre-change answer that opened with "the CSO Data Room", named an agent to the user, and routed the VP to "whoever manages Data Room content updates".
- `quantitative_content_present` **true**, evidence *"55% utilization in Q3 2025; industry benchmarks showing typical utilization ranges of 40–60%"*. Both figures are real, correctly sourced, and neither is a South Korea co-op rate. Owen's own overall verdict was `pass: true`; only the class failed it.

**John's decision, 2026-07-29 — a §8 baseline change to §5b, approved in conversation.** Scope the criterion to the asked-for quantity, and stop instructing Marcus Webb — GEO CSO Expert to avoid numbers. §5b's own regression example was already this narrow — *"an answer that suddenly reports a South Korea co-op utilization number"* — while the criterion it sat next to was not.

**Who makes the judgment matters.** The driver cannot decide whether a quoted figure answers the question; that needs reading. So Owen gains a fifth criterion and judges it. **He is deliberately never told which questions carry the `honest-gap` class** — a judge told the expected outcome grades toward it. He sees `question` and `artifact_content` exactly as today and answers a narrower question; the driver alone knows the class and decides what to do with his answer.

**Not in scope, and not caused by this.** Case 12 (`vietnam-reseller`) will still FAIL after this patch: its review extension produces a theory-test artifact from Priya that **Owen fails on his ordinary rich-answer rubric** (`pass: false`, platform-language narration in the artifact itself). That is a real content defect on a different agent's artifact, independent of this class and of any scoring change — the design session files it separately. `AGT-36` closes on the class scoring correctly and Marcus's gap answer clearing all of §5b's bars on both questions, which is exactly what §5b said up front (*"Deliberately does not turn either case green"*).
