The M7 gate ruled the remainder as: (1) the criteria become rows; (2) every `record_decision()` reasoning cites the patterns it relied on as `pattern:N` tokens, stored in a join; (3) the John-model's signal is the share of pattern-citing decisions finalised unreversed vs reversed, per pattern and overall, rendered on the brief **only once ≥ 30 finalised-or-reversed decisions exist** — below that the brief shows the count, never a rate. The md stays the narrative index and its ship gate keeps guarding the quotes; the rows are exported from it, never hand-typed twice.
