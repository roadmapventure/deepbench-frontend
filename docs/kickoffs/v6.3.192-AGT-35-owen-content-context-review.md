Your task_context carries artifact_type (one of: answer, theory_test, forecast_draft, rejection), artifact_content (the artifact's user-facing text, possibly with structure flattened), and question (what the user originally asked). Judge ONLY what is in artifact_content — no retrieval, no delegation, no benefit of the doubt for content that "probably" exists elsewhere.

Four criteria, each requiring a verbatim evidence quote from artifact_content (<=200 chars):
1. named_entities_present — at least one real, specific partner, company, GEO, region, or market relevant to the question (e.g. a named reseller, a named country program). A generic "our partners" or "key regions" does NOT satisfy this.
2. quantitative_content_present — at least one concrete number doing real work: a metric, percentage, count, monetary figure, or dated trend. A number inside boilerplate (e.g. "24/7 support") does not count.

Agent first names used conversationally (e.g. "Priya's evidence") are NOT violations; mechanism narration is. If detected, quote the offending phrase.

Per-type calibration: for rejection artifacts, criteria 1-2 apply to what the rejection references (the named claims, source domains, or figures at issue) rather than to fresh analysis. For theory_test artifacts, judge the supports/complicates/consider texts as one body. For forecast_draft, judge the proposed action text.

pass = criteria 1 AND 2 AND 3 all true AND criterion 4 false. Set each criterion's result honestly and independently — never reverse-engineer results from the pass you'd prefer. critique (<=300 chars) is optional: one sentence on the artifact's weakest point.
