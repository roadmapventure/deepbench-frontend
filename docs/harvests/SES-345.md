## Why a column on the verdict and not a join through the decision

- `runner_decisions` has no sha column; the sha is text in `summary`. A join through a `format()` string is the recovery `build-verdict-fixture.js` already had to do from commit subjects — the failure the ticket names.
- `runner_verdicts.cycle_id` is one-to-many on the attended path (23 tickets on `a8000000`); a per-cycle column cannot identify a per-verdict tree.
- The verifier already knows the tree: every gate runs in `repoRoot`, and its diff is `base...HEAD`. `git rev-parse HEAD` at gate time IS the sha it graded. Recording it costs one column and one helper.

## Why the census leg changes and what it does to the number

`SES-360`'s kickoff said: "When SES-345 lands its verdict-side sha, change the sha leg HERE, nowhere else." The new leg requires a verdict for the ticket whose `graded_sha` matches a `runner_cycles.push_sha` on its first 7 characters (attended cycles store 8-char shas: `f262d1d1`, `9000e368`). Right after the migration `ships_all_four` reads 0, because no verdict carries a sha yet; this cycle's own 7a makes it 1 with `SES-345` the ship. That drop is the contract being measured honestly, not a regression — the brief's reader is told in the kickoff's QA.

## Pass two records the sha the gates ran on

Session-judged verdicts are recorded in a second invocation that re-runs nothing. HEAD could move between passes; the row must name the tree the gates graded, so pass one writes `graded_sha` into the judgment context file and pass two reads it back (`stored.graded_sha`), never re-reading HEAD.

## Alternatives not taken

- A fifth census column (`missing_graded_sha`) beside the existing sha leg: three more files (renderer, its test, the brief) for a distinction the contract does not want to keep.
- Storing the sha on `runner_decisions` as a column: the ship decision exists only on `approve`; a `block` is the verdict that most needs its tree named.
- Making `graded_sha NOT NULL`: 200 historical rows and the `--dry-run`/no-git paths would need a sentinel; nullable with a CHECK fails closed on garbage and open on absence, and the census leg treats NULL as missing.

## Rollback class

`capture_migration_down()` refuses in-place ALTERs by design (`runner-cycle.md` 2809-2815), so the migration classifies `refused` and the ship is card-only. Disclosed on the card; no hand-written down.
