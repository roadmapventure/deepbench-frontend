## Why mechanism, and what it costs

- No function means nothing new in `pg_proc`, nothing born with EXECUTE for `anon` and `authenticated` (`.claude/rules/supabase-column-grants.md`, 2026-09-02 addendum; the old build's own probe `agt459_probe()` is still live with those grants, `AGT-549`), no overload to drop, no migration down to capture.
- Code and data move independently: the push carries no schema dependency, and the data close is one decision that `reverse_decision()` restores whole. `runner_items` is on its allow-list and Guard B refuses only `gated_before_build` cards and the acting cycle's own.
- The shape is not new: `settle-ship.js writeCost()` already does decision -> image -> PATCH on a ship card over REST.
- COST, stated: the three REST calls of one chunk are not one transaction. An image POST followed by a failed PATCH leaves images whose restore is a no-op. A card decided by someone else between the GET and the PATCH makes the PATCH come back short and the script throws, naming the decision, rather than leave an image that could undo another writer's decision silently. No one has decided a ship card since 2026-09-14.

## Calls I made (reversible, JOHN-0925-DESIGNER-DECIDES)

1. **Done and removed only (192), not the ticket's 221.** John's rule, live in `dm-audit-review-intent` and `ga-review-intent`: "(a) its ticket is already done or removed -- retire the report as a receipt, nothing to decide". `removal proposed` is not removed: the same Skill has the Development Manager approve or reject each proposal, and a rejected one goes back to `open`. Those 20 close on a later sweep once ruled. The 9 with no ticket are reports on John's directives (DIR-60d35b49, the offsite backup, the M4/M5 succession, two step-4 fixes, two gate-ruling reports): no clause of the rule names them, so they are filed as a finding, not closed by my reading (pattern:157, pattern:166).
2. **Two columns, no `decision_reason`.** `trg_runner_items_citations` fires on `UPDATE OF ... decision_reason` and its block 3 appends reversal-handle sentences to `plain_worth` (about one unlisted handle per card on the 212 with a ticket, measured by SELECT over the stale set, 347 ms). Not writing the column means the trigger does not fire and the write is exactly what the risk line says. The reason lives on the `card-retire` decision each image points at.
3. **The sweep never fails a settle.** A sweep that did not run leaves today's state and the next delivering cycle retries. Settling the ticket is the script's primary job (the same argument `writeCost()` makes).
4. **Backfill after the push.** Once the code is on dev any cycle's settle would sweep, so the Builder runs the full close itself right after its push, under its own cycle id. Before the push would leave retired cards under the old `decision=not.is.null` reader.
