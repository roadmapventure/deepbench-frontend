## 3. What was considered and dropped

- **Keep the ticket key, pick the card whose `created_at` is nearest the cycle.** Rejected: `cycle_id` is present and exact; a nearest-timestamp heuristic is a guess where a join exists (pattern:3 — reproducible output over convenient implementation).
- **Fall back to the ticket's newest card when a cycle has no card.** Rejected by the ticket and by pattern:34 — a borrowed sentence is worse than a blank, and AGT-117's item-less mid-build cycles make the blank a real, frequent state.
- **Warn instead of refusing.** Rejected: the ticket says refuse, and a warning on a generated file that nobody reads at render time is a warning nobody sees. The cost is named in the kickoff's decision (a).
- **A new test file.** Rejected on the 3-file cap: SES-177's size-clause fixture keys `cards` on `"SES-381"` and breaks under the new key, so that file is being edited regardless (pattern:17).
- **Grade all ten fetched rows, not the three rendered.** Rejected: three bullets reach disk; refusing a render over a row that is not in the file would block close-outs for no reader's benefit.
