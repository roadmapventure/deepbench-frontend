## 3. THE CALL (JOHN-0925-DESIGNER-DECIDES; reversible, recorded here and in the kickoff's STOP LINE)

The ticket offers two fixes. I took a narrow form of both and rejected the wide form of one.

- **Rejected: append a ticket's held source to the shared `candidates` pool.** One line, no validator change, no prompt
  change — and it makes a `kind='scope'` decision citable by every other ticket on the list, which is exactly what
  `CANDIDATE_FILTER` was added to prevent (AGT-432, directive `0078af4d`): "only the `john-ruling` ones that stand are
  John's own words — the rest are the agents' records of calls already made." Weakening that without John's words is
  pattern:166, and `agt-281`'s live arm asserts `johnKeys.length === rulings` precisely so nobody can.
- **Rejected: relax the membership rule alone.** It opens the door, but she would then echo a key whose text she was never
  shown and score it 0-5 blind — a grade with no readable source behind it (pattern:33), and her own `method` tells her to
  read the candidate behind a key before citing it.
- **Taken: give the list door the shape the single-ticket door already has.** `--prepare` hands a ticket its own source AND
  that row's columns (`buildTaskContext`'s `need_source` / `need_source_row`); the list door hands the key but not the text.
  So: each proposal carries `need_source_head` (the same `CANDIDATE_HEAD` column, cut at `HEAD_CHARS`), and the membership
  rule accepts the one value that cannot lose anything — the ticket's own held key (`src !== held && !keys.has(src)`).
  The pool stays John's rulings only; the echo stays mandatory; nothing else may be cited (pattern:17, pattern:14, pattern:65).
- **The prompt row moves with the code** (pattern:163): without a line in `vc-reorganize-intent.method`, a ticket whose
  held key is not in the pool reads to her as a ticket with no candidate, and her step 2 turns that into `not-needed` —
  which is a `removal proposed` write on a ticket John himself named. That is the one dangerous failure mode of the code-only
  fix, and it is why the migration is in scope.
