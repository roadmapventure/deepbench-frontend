## Build: the additions

1. **Finding rows beyond the watch's inputs.** Every Verifier finding, every block the Builder proves pre-existing, every `runner_card_asks` row and every Ticket Owner judgment finding (SES-385) becomes a finding row the manager decides.
2. **The call, before every pick, chained cycles included.** *Fix now*: epic, `scope_rationale` and `supports_*`, pinned first in the queue. *Later*: epic and `scope_rationale`, normal rank. *John*: only when a John ruling is needed. One reversible decision per call.
3. **Reuse.** A finding that matches an open ticket anywhere on the board uses that ticket; no duplicate is filed.
4. **Pull-in** (John: "keep point 2"). A matched ticket parked in a planned or paused project that running work depends on is moved into the executing project by the manager, with the reason logged.
5. **Rulings.** A new John ruling that relies on something working triggers a search for open tickets saying that thing is broken, and pulls them forward.
6. **The Auditor on demand.** A finding that two rule homes disagree has the manager call the Auditor's `audit-governance-corpus` on that one topic in the same cycle, and decide from its `proposed_resolution`.
7. **Attended-only fixes** (writes under `.claude/`, rule B39) are named in the manager's report and in `public.project_blockers`.
8. **Approvals are the manager's** (John, item 4). Re-opening a delivered ticket whose run named unbuilt work, homing a discovered ticket, pulling in a parked ticket, the queue order among them, and retiring a superseded or false card take no card and no question to John.
9. **Walls.** Only inside the executing project's charter and the budget and pace walls. The manager never builds, never grades, and never changes a John ruling.
10. **No early stop.** The chain may not end `nothing_pickable` while a finding row awaits a call.
11. **Order.** Edits to the manager's own Skill rows are build work under AGENT-ROW-AGREED-TICKET, and they come after SES-364 so they are reversible.
