## 2. The design and the alternatives not taken

Chosen: a generic `--prior-proposals=<found_by>` flag on `scripts/agent-prompt.js` that attaches `task_context.prior_proposals` from `audit_findings` (`finding_type = proposal`, `found_by = <value>`), plus two Skill-row sentences telling the turn what the key is and to drop a match, plus the runbook command gaining the flag.

- Why a flag keyed on a `found_by` VALUE and not on the capability slug: `.claude/rules/capabilities-are-data.md` and §19b forbid `if (capability_slug === 'x')`; the SES-395 degrade in this same file fences by equality for the same reason. Any lane with a `found_by` can use it; nothing in the script names the Researcher.
- Why the Skill rows change too (pattern:7): an undocumented key in task_context is a hint, not an instruction. The 2026-10-17 turn already *wanted* this data (it asked the ruling to check). The sentence makes the drop a stated behaviour; the data edit is reversible (before-images on `runner_before_images`, cycle-attributed) and mirrored into `docs/design/ga-agents-seed.sql` so a re-seed does not lose it (pattern:163).
- Why `finding_type = proposal` only (pattern:90): the ticket words it so. Observed residue, reported not filed: this lane also holds 14 `ticketed` `defect`/`gap` rows; a re-derivation of one of those would not be caught by this guard. AGT-371 (board-write dedupe, open) is the layer for that.
- Why fail closed on a failed read (pattern:165): an empty `prior_proposals` that was never read is indistinguishable from "nothing filed" and reproduces the defect exactly; exit 2 is the honest answer, matching the script's existing credential refusals.
- Not taken: pasting the rows into the runbook (pattern:93 — the rows move); a deterministic post-turn dedupe in the routine (that is AGT-371's layer); a new capability or schema (none needed); editing the `RESEARCHER-ROUTINE-PROMPT` block (it is the RemoteTrigger's byte-identical copy and John's switch).
