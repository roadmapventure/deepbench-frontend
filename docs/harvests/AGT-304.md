## 4. Why this shape and not another

- **Not a loosening.** agt-245 keeps every ancestor assertion for every reachable pair, gains an accounting assert (`checked + unreachable.length === resolved.length`) that no silent skip survives, and FAILS outright if the clone is complete and the pairs are still missing — which is the only reading under which 0 reachable pairs is a cohort defect. agt-151's regex widens by one alternative and gains a per-line proof: every `?` must be backed by a `push_sha` the checkout cannot reach, and every named base must be backed by one it can (or by no `push_sha` at all, the `started_at` fallback path). The old line asserted nothing about the recorded sha.
- **A script-only fix was rejected.** Making `baseShaFor()` fall back to `started_at` when `push_sha` is unreachable cannot help: the shallow floor is 2026-10-07 and `rev-list --before=2026-10-06T04:39Z origin/dev` is empty, and at CI's depth 1 all three jobs read `?`. A green here and a red in CI is the failure mode this ticket exists to remove (pattern:3).
- **Two suites, one cause, one slice** — John's 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-26 rulings. Nothing else in the 34 shares it: the other fails are board state, live counts, project state, a wedged finaliser, an absent anon key, or the two left red by ruling.
- **Left red by ruling, said out loud in the kickoff's QA and STOP LINE:** `agt-291-finish-sensor` and `agt-184-ledger-orphan`. They clear when the connector's migration-apply path does (AGT-495 / AGT-465). This slice applies no migration and makes no Supabase write, so the live 60s write ceiling cannot touch its delivery.
