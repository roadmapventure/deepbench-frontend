## 3. Why three routines, not one and not five

- The ticket's own Fix line says "do the same for every routine whose live prompt carries the stop". A ticket closes on every sentence, so the auditor alone would not close it.
- Researcher and market are the urgent two. Their runbooks say John pastes the block into the routine (`researcher-routine.md:38`, `market-agent.md:70`). Pasted as they stand today, each would delete his own stop from a live routine. That is the real harm here; the weekly drift finding is only its sensor.
- The three share one byte-identical paragraph, so one literal, one helper and one test prove all three.
- `victoria-reorg` and `model-watch` run different wordings, and `model-watch`'s presupposes a schedule-gate paragraph that is also live-only. Each needs its own literal and, for `model-watch`, a call on whether the schedule gate enters the block. `AGT-517` is open and already owns that; I reused it instead of duplicating it. After this ships, `AGT-517`'s remainder is those two blocks.

## 4. The needs-desktop ruling (D1)

The flag says no unattended cycle should pick the ticket up because the fix "needs an attended session at the machine that can read and write the routine". Against the evidence:

- The fix edits tracked files. No step writes a routine; the live routine already runs the stop.
- No step needs to read a routine either. The paragraph is in the tracked tree (13 files hold it) and in the ledger's own finding rows.
- I read the live prompts from a cloud session with a read-only tool, so even the design-time read did not need John's machine.

Ruling: `needs-desktop` is mis-set on `AGT-567`; an unattended cycle can build this. The same reasoning applies to `AGT-517` (its Fix is also a paste into runbook blocks). It does not apply to `AGT-263`, whose Fix is a push to the live runner routine -- that one is genuinely attended.

Nothing in the design edits a live routine's stored prompt, writes under `.claude/`, touches `docs/runbooks/runner-cycle.md` (so `cycle-card.md`'s pinned hash is unaffected and no re-render is needed), or merges to main.
