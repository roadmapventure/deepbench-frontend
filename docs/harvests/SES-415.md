# SES-415 / SES-416 / SES-417 — what this week's Moat Support tickets teach the staff

**Requested by John, 2026-09-16, verbatim:** "can you use the reasoning from new tickets that grew from an original and new tickets not from an original, why they were created, why they were deferred, why they were removed, and use that reasoning to make the designer, builder, and dev manager smarter and more autonomous, and also to build out more reasoning logic defined, documented, used, accessed?"

**What this file is:** the starting analysis written by attended session `reasoning-0916` from the live board, measured 2026-09-16 around 14:30 CT. The Designer verifies every claim, grounds each criterion in verbatim quotes the way `SES-004` did (`scripts/check-decision-pattern-quotes.js`), and turns it into rows. Nothing here is a criterion until it is a row in `public.decision_patterns`.

**The corpus:** the 49 tickets added to project `moat-support` after it was created on 2026-09-11. The 15 originals were SES-343, SES-344, SES-345, SES-348, SES-352, SES-353, SES-354, SES-355, SES-358, SES-359, SES-360, LOG-149, AGT-69, AGT-70 and SES-361. The corpus covers each ticket's "why" text, the deferral reasons in `public.project_blockers`, and the two removals. Of the 49, 18 grew out of an original and 31 did not. 7 tickets are deferred across the project and 2 are removed.

## Finding 0 — the reasoning library exists, but the staff never sees it

- `public.decision_patterns` holds 162 criteria in 11 sections. They are exported to `docs/JOHN-DECISION-PATTERNS.md` by `scripts/export-decision-patterns.js`, and every recorded decision cites them as `pattern:N` in `public.runner_decision_patterns`.
- **Defined, not maintained:** no row has been added or changed since 2026-09-03.
- **Used, barely:** 34 of 162 criteria have ever been cited. There are 271 citations. 74 of them are `pattern:0` ("no standing pattern applied"), and 76 are one criterion, #137. The section "The user's experience" (28 criteria) has never been cited.
- **Not accessed:** no Skill row of any governance agent mentions `decision_patterns` or a `pattern:N`. That covers the prefixes `ds-*`, `bd-*`, `dm-*`, `vf-*`, `pz-*`, `rs-*`, `au-*` and `to-*`. The Designer, the Builder and the Development Manager are assembled without the library, so it cannot shape a kickoff, a build or a pick.

## Eight lessons, each a candidate criterion, with its evidence and the agent that must apply it

**L1. A check grades the change, never the live world.** Applies to: the Designer (writes the QA), the Builder (proves a red is not its own), and the Development Manager and Verifier (separate outside causes).
Evidence:
- SES-371: the test went red when a 7-day window aged out.
- SES-386 and SES-401: guards graded board state and an empty queue.
- SES-404: a test asserts on the live corpus.
- SES-405: a hard-coded two-ticket list.
- SES-393: evidence rebuilt from git history that CI's shallow clone lacks.
- SES-380 and SES-381: a generated file's size and drift.
- SES-382: shared fixtures collide under parallel cycles.
- SES-403: ships blocked for outside causes had no exit but John.

Criterion: a test reads live state only if it declares NOT RUN when that state is absent. Before a red is called the ship's, it is run on the unchanged tree.

**L2. A change has more than one home, and one ship changes all of them.** Applies to: the Designer.
Evidence:
- SES-410: the function, runbook, test oracle and the Development Manager's card copy all carried the removed stop, and the kickoff missed the copy.
- SES-400: registry, runbook and snapshot disagreed.
- SES-411: a live rule contradicted the function with no red.
- SES-399, SES-364 and SES-407: twin undo allowlists and before-image keys.
- SES-408: the environment register and the Designer's Knowledge row.
- SES-381: rendered versus committed state.

Criterion: the kickoff lists every home of what it changes, and the build changes them together. The homes include registry + snapshot, runbook + card + `dm-knowledge-cycle-card`, function + test oracle, and twin allowlists.

**L3. Know the environment before designing.** Applies to: the Designer (reads the register) and the Builder (adds what it learns).
Evidence:
- SES-393: CI clones shallow.
- SES-406: a laptop path runs in the cloud.
- SES-379: the Builder pushes before the verifier runs, so the git diff is empty.
- SES-367: the session prompt path lacked an injected field.
- SES-412: a working Builder writes no heartbeat, so the stall alarm lies.
- SES-396: 6 of the 9 tickets the runner filed on 2026-09-12 were environment facts.

Criterion: design against `docs/runbooks/environment-facts.md`. A build that discovers a fact appends it, which is SES-408.

**L4. A budget rule is only as good as its sensor.** Applies to: the Designer and the Development Manager.
Evidence:
- SES-389: a 22-hour-old reading was trusted.
- SES-374, SES-388 and SES-398: the reader depended on John's laptop.
- SES-390 and SES-395: a second meter (Fable) was ignored.
- SES-409: cycles hand-estimate tokens about six times low.
- SES-392: the laptop reader fails silently (deferred).

Criterion: a gate on a number checks the number's age and source and fails closed. Usage is taken from recorded figures, never estimated.

**L5. A John rule is never removed or weakened without his words naming that change. Adding a degrade does not remove a stop.** Applies to: all agents and every attended session.
Evidence: SES-410 (John: "i never said remove the stop.") and SES-411.
Criterion: John is quoted only for what he said. A change that weakens a live rule cites the rule and his words, or it does not ship.

**L6. Decisions belong to the staff. John keeps only money, production releases, and hiring or switching on agents.** Applies to: the Development Manager.
Evidence:
- SES-413: John's rule, 2026-09-16.
- SES-378, SES-402 and SES-397: the staff cannot yet decide.
- SES-394: an agreed agent was gated three times.
- SES-385: a half-shipped ticket kept a stale design flag.
- SES-403: blocked ships waited on John.
- 98 undecided cards on 2026-09-16.

Criterion: decide and record the undo. A build's finding is decided before the next pick:
- fix now under the same ticket when it is that ticket's own residue;
- later, with a scope reason and a home;
- or drop, with the evidence.

**L7. What a fresh agent must read has a byte budget.** Applies to: the Designer and the Development Manager.
Evidence: SES-376 (kickoffs of 18-56 KB), SES-377 (a 365 KB runbook re-read each cycle) and SES-409.
Criterion: a kickoff is at most 8 KB, and a cycle reads the card, not the runbook. Reasoning goes to the harvest.

**L8. Every recorded promise is keepable.** Applies to: the Builder.
Evidence: SES-399 (an undo could not restore a backfill), SES-407 (418 before-images keyed by ticket text) and SES-364.
Criterion: a before-image's `pk_value` is the row's uuid. A table outside `reversible_tables()` gets no undo promise.

## Why tickets were deferred — criteria for the Development Manager

| Reason | Tickets | Revisit trigger on record |
|---|---|---|
| Outside the agreed completion set | SES-382, SES-391, SES-392 | "after the Moat build starts"; "main cause gone under the pre-approval ruling"; "GitHub Actions is the primary sensor" |
| A lane paused on purpose until a date | SES-369 | invention paused 7 days on 2026-09-11, **revisit 2026-09-18** |
| John's own lane | SES-361 | he runs the P2/P4 definitions separately |
| Low impact, no trigger recorded | LOG-150, SES-380 | none |

Criterion: a deferral records its revisit trigger, and the manager re-checks the triggers weekly. When a trigger fires, the manager un-defers the ticket without asking.

## Why tickets were removed

| Reason | Ticket | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The goal was achieved another way | SES-358 | the pre-flight was proven by five live ships |
| Superseded by a ticket closer to the root cause | SES-372 | replaced by SES-373 |

Criterion: a ticket is removed only with the evidence, or with the superseding ticket named.

## Where each agent falls short today

- **The Designer:** its Knowledge is the standard plus environment facts. Nothing covers L1, L2, L5 or L7, and it does not cite patterns.
- **The Builder:** it has no "prove a red is pre-existing" rule, no L8 rule, and no citations.
- **The Development Manager:** it hands out the pick only. It has no fix-now, later or drop criteria, no deferral or removal criteria, and no citations.

## The build, in three tickets

- **SES-415, mining pass 2.** The lessons and the deferral and removal criteria become grounded rows in a new section. Each row carries a tag for the agent that applies it. The doc is exported and the quote check stays green.
- **SES-416, read and cite.** The Designer, the Builder and the Development Manager are assembled with the rows tagged for their role, rendered inline and within a byte budget. Every answer names the patterns it applied, and the citations are recorded per agent run.
- **SES-417, keep learning.** The manager tags every discovered, deferred or removed ticket with its root-cause pattern. A cause seen three times without a pattern becomes a draft criterion that the manager adopts as an undoable decision. The Auditor reports criteria never cited in 30 days, and failures that repeated even though a criterion existed.
