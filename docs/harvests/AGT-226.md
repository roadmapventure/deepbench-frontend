## The record cannot tell you what the fact was — and that is not this ticket's to fix

24 of the 26 rows carry the detail string `build needed a fact the kickoff did not carry`, verbatim,
because `docs/runbooks/runner-cycle.md:2964` hands the Builder that exact `--detail`. The fixed
wording is deliberate: `staff-watch.js`'s fingerprint is `sha256(agent|kind|normalize(detail))` and a
per-cycle detail fingerprints a new way every run and never reaches `PROMOTION_BAR = 3`. So making
the detail carry the fact would take promotions to zero and orphan the open `runner_card_asks` rows
keyed on `e7393ed419f16617`. Not designed here. The facts were recovered from `runner_cycles.notes`
instead.

   kickoffs: 327 citations, 322 gradeable, and it would have refused exactly **2** — both from
   months ago, **neither from this week**. AGT-167 carries three citations and all three resolve;
   AGT-169 and AGT-245 carry none. Rejected: a gate whose green grades nothing that bit (§19v).
2. **Infer anchors from prose** (`` `old` `` → `` `new` `` pairs). Probe over the 60 newest kickoffs:
   377 candidate anchors, **201 unresolvable at HEAD** — a 53% false-positive rate, driven by markdown
   escaping, prose arrows and elided paths. Rejected: unusable as a refusal.
3. **Declared anchors, resolved** — chosen. It is SES-359's shape replayed: the Designer declares,
   the checker grades the declaration. SES-359 is the proof it works — every kickoff since carries a
   `Lanes:` line, and AGT-187 later closed the hole where the caller supplied the line itself.
4. **Teach the Designer through its Intent** (`ds-kickoff-intent.traits.schema`). Refused, and not by
   preference: it is an ACTIVE agent's `public.skill_profiles` row, which
   `.claude/rules/agent-roster-inert.md` and §19v P5 place beyond every rung. SES-359 held exactly
   this ask ("the Designer is asked for the line by the checker rather than by its own Intent until
   that lands") and SES-376 got the same answer at rung 21. Filed direction, not reached for.
5. **A new `--json` kind plus a runbook branch.** Refused on bytes: `runner-cycle.md` is 380,862 of a

   — costs nothing and the existing step-6 branch already re-assembles once and records the finding.

## What this does NOT do, stated

- It does not grade a fact the kickoff never declared. A Designer that declares nothing gets a green
  that says out loud it graded nothing (`anchors_graded:false`). Flipping absence into a refusal is a
  follow-up, once the corpus shows every assembly carries a block; doing it in this ship would refuse
  every in-flight kickoff and wedge the drain chain.
- It does not catch a wrong exit code, a live CHECK constraint the kickoff never mentions, or a §6
  that contradicts §2. Those need a reader, not a resolver.
- The count is **files**, not occurrences, because the failure it exists for was a wrong file list.
