This is pattern:1 (the collision is gone, not linted) and pattern:17 (the sibling scripts' `--out` already means "the JSON"). 3 files.

I am flagging this plainly: it satisfies the ticket's goal and its first sentence ("eliminate the collision"), and it replaces fix (2) and the optional "refuse a `.json` `--out`" rather than performing them.
