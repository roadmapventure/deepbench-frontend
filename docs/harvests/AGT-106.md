## Why a reviewed-postings filter and not "every posting"

- The ticket says "posting (all statuses)". Read literally against match-finder, that sentence already conflicts with AGT-154's pinned `status === 'new'` filter and the `match-finder-filter-still-narrower` arm, so the parenthetical is the author's contrast with that filter ("do not hide the reviewed rows"), not an acceptance criterion in its own right.
- Both Intents select in-prompt: "posting records … that carry pay" and "postings past their verdict". Every row carrying pay or a verdict is a reviewed record; no raw card carries either. Excluding the 148 cards loses nothing either Intent uses and saves 1,010 KB per render — for growth-review, a weekly scheduled run whose prompt would otherwise reach ~1.9 MB.
- `status !== 'new'` is the structural discriminator by construction: `--fetch-postings` and `--fetch-linkedin-alerts`/alert review write `status:'new'`; posting-review and market-watch rewrite the record with pay and verdict and a later status. It is the same mechanism (`KIND_FILTERS`, keyed by capability since AGT-154) with the complementary predicate, not a content judgment (pattern:17, pattern:18). A predicate on `pay_min`/`verdict` presence would encode the Intent's wording into code and drift the day the Intent changes.
- Alternatives set aside: (a) no filter — literal, +1.2 MB per render, rejected on cost with nothing gained; (b) `pay_min != null || verdict != null` — 90 KB, but two clauses copied from prose; (c) amending the Intents to stop promising the kinds — finding b115bc51's second option; rejected because the kinds exist and the capabilities are for grading against them (P1 - Improves John's Skills).
