## 3. Where the live defect actually is

### 3a. The runbook restates the flat pair to itself — unfiled until now

`docs/runbooks/runner-cycle.md:2825-2826`:

> Implement within the scope caps (one item, ≤3 files, ≤4 tasks).

That sits **125 lines after** the same file's step 5a at `:2700-2708`, which says the caps *"are
no longer flat: they are a baseline plus one file and one task per rung"*. It is the sentence the
build step recites, it is not John's file, and it is not governed content — so it is squarely a
cycle's to fix, and fixing it is the whole of *"stop the flat pair travelling, without touching
your rule."* This is the ticket's operative defect and no row named it.

### 3b. Nothing grades or quotes the computed pair

`extra_files` / `extra_tasks` appear in `scripts/verifier.js` **twice, both in comments**
(`:498`, `:500` — *"part (c)'s"*). The live `class_autonomy` row is fetched at `:2938` and only
`auto_done` is read from it (`:304-312` explains why the comparison must not be re-derived). So
no verdict has ever printed the pair it graded a ship against — ticket arm (b), alive.

### 3c. Arm (a) already holds structurally; what fails is compliance

Step 6 (`:2758`) and step 7 (`:2903`) both carry `caps:{"files":N,"tasks":M}` in `task_context`,
filled from step 5a's call. This very kickoff received `files 22 / tasks 23` that way. The
failure mode on cycle `40d8dfe3` was not a missing mechanism: that cycle's `notes` carries **no
cap line at all** (read this cycle — `tokens_at_open`, stamp match, runbook bytes, branch, 4a CI
red, and nothing else). Measured across the board: **101 of 512** cycles started in the last 30
days carry a `files N (+k)` line in `notes`; 35 carry empty notes. A prose instruction obeyed
one time in five is a structural problem (pattern:10), but it is a different one, so it is filed
rather than folded in.

### 3d. Arm (c) is forbidden to a cycle, not merely delicate

`docs/STANDARDS.md:60` records the `SES-122`(c) ruling verbatim:

> **`HR-SCOPE`** … `CLAUDE.md` is John's file, so the row now points at the doc a ticket is
> allowed to reconcile; his hard-rule `Scope` line stays exactly as he wrote it and is the
> **baseline** the amended row cites — cited, never contradicted, and **never edited by a cycle**.

The ticket's arm (c) asks for *"one pointer sentence"* appended at `CLAUDE.md:57`. That is an
edit to John's line, and a shipped governance decision says a cycle does not make it. The ticket
already sensed the boundary (*"rewording or lifting the hard rule itself is your word"*) but drew
it one notch too permissively: the ruling covers the pointer too, not only the reword.

This is therefore the one thing on this ticket that is John's, and it is put to him as a single
yes/no `runner_questions` row rather than decided. It is a genuinely open call because both
answers are defensible: leaving `CLAUDE.md:57` bare keeps his file pristine at the cost of every
cold session reading *"Max 3 files"* as a ceiling with no pointer; appending one sentence fixes
the reading at the cost of a sentence in his file that he did not write.

## 4. Alternatives considered and rejected

- **Change `class_autonomy()` so the ladder keys off the change rather than the ticket's class.**
  Rejected. The runbook states the grant is *"a fact about the class, never about the epic or the
  ticket"* (`:2712`), the thresholds are stored columns with one home (`SES-146`), and the
  function returned correct answers in both cycles. Re-keying the ladder is a policy change to
  John's cap model, not a defect fix, and nothing in the evidence says the class key is wrong —
  only that the *cost* it is measured against is lumpy, which is 3c/AGT-229 territory.
- **Fix the lumpy cost — make a runbook byte stop dragging three pins.** That is exactly
  `AGT-229` (filed today, cycle `4ac5460e`), whose own text measures it: *"the kickoff named 5
  files and the Builder had to touch 8, the three extra being exactly those pins."* Refiling its
  acceptance criterion under this number would violate pattern:95. This ship pays the coupling
  once and leaves the removal to its owner.
- **Edit `CLAUDE.md:57` anyway, since STANDARDS.md §2 already calls it a restatement.** Rejected
  on 3d. A ruling that says *"never edited by a cycle"* is not satisfied by a cycle judging its
  own edit harmless (pattern:166 — a John rule is not weakened without his words).
- **Also make the cap line in `notes` self-writing.** Rejected as scope: it is a second mechanism
  (3c), it is not any of the ticket's three arms, and the ticket closes without it.
