The finding was a string match. Reading each occurrence in context:

The body says "every cell is lead, partial, none or not-our-fight with its reason and source in data.matrix" — the matrix IS the record. - **`f22e4c8d`** (31 hits), **`e014e2cb`** (1), **`8f683e41`** (10 body + 3 data) — every occurrence is a source-access date (`[src:https://claude.com/pricing 2026-10-05, page read]`, `[src:task input research brief 2026-10-05]`, "unchanged from the 2026-10-05 record") or a scan window ("2026-09-28 to 2026-10-05"). ## 4. The calls I made (JOHN-0925-DESIGNER-DECIDES), and what each does not do

1. **Three corrected, seven held.** Does not: remove the literals from the seven rows' stored bodies/data. They stay as quotations of the wrong value, source dates and one matrix cell, none rendered since AGT-550. A later auditor string-match will see them again — and will now also see the `data.recheck` stamp saying why they stand.
2. **`2026-10-05`'s acceptance restated.** The ticket says zero in the render. Two hits are `created_at` of true rows. The kickoff asserts zero in every rendered title and head and reports the two `date` hits. Does not: reach a literal zero. The alternative (retire + replace `f22e4c8d` and `8f683e41` so their replacements get today's `created_at`) hides a creation date behind a fake correction and loses their `data` (sources, copy_tests, buyer profile fields). Rejected.
3. **C1/C2 bodies kept.** The writer coalesces the row's body when no `body` key is passed; both bodies are free of the name. Does not: reconcile `22215386`'s "Enterprise since 2026-09-03" sentence with the "waitlisted" title — not the fact the ticket names, and AGT-470 CALL 7/8 set the same title over a body of similar shape.
4. **Every `reason` begins `AGT-566 sweep`**, because the writer hard-codes `'AGT-470'` as both the `record_decision` handle and `data.recheck.ticket`. The sweep's rows and decisions are therefore found by the reason prefix, and none of the reasons may carry one of the three literals (the stamp lands in `data`, and a reason quoting the wrong string would put it back into the row). This is also why T2's assertions key on `reason`, not `ticket`.

Cheapest variant: ten calls to the shipped writer and one guard. No migration (no `ALTER`, no new function, no handle parameter), no change to `scripts/market-agent.js`, no new kind. Rejected alternatives: (a) a new overload of `apply_claim_recheck` taking the handle — a migration plus `.claude/rules/supabase-function-signature.md`'s drop-the-stale-overload dance for a stamp; (b) direct `PATCH` of `market_records` — forbidden by the Researcher routine's own sentence and by the kickoff that shipped it; (c) a `correction`-kind row — a different channel (capability-scoped, read by `readRecords()` separately), not a correction of the record.

## 5. Execution path and idempotency

`execute_sql` over the Supabase MCP ran `pg_get_functiondef` this turn, so the cycle has it; `POST rpc/apply_claim_recheck` with `{p_cycle, p_session: null, p}` is the same function over PostgREST (the agt-470 guard's `rpc()` uses it). Each call is one transaction. The function does not check the target row's `status`, so a second run of a `corrected` call against an already-retired row inserts a SECOND replacement and breaks T2's "exactly one" — hence "run each once, record its uuid" and the STOP clause.
