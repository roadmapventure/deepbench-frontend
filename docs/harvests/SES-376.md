## Alternatives weighed

1. **Enforce in `check-session-docs.js` as a new check.** Rejected: the script's gating set is 9/10/11 by design (SES-199) and its header forbids widening it; a non-gating WARN would be advisory, and the ticket asks for a refusal. It would also scan all 773 historical kickoffs and need a version floor.
2. **Enforce as a fourth mechanical gate in `verifier.js`'s `GATES`.** Rejected: `verdictRowFor` stores per-gate columns; a fourth key is a `runner_verdicts` schema change the ticket did not ask for. The chosen shape — a `--check-kickoff` mode for step 6 (before the build ever reads it) plus a verdict-path block when `--kickoff` is handed in — refuses at the right moment and records at the ledger without a new column.
3. **JSON-schema `maxLength` alone.** Rejected as the only gate: `maxLength` counts characters, the session lane renders the schema as prose the model may miss, and nothing mechanical would refuse. Kept as the row-level statement of the contract, with the byte check as the enforcement.
4. **A retry loop at step 6.** Chosen at exactly one retry with `over_cap` in `task_context`, then `gated_before_build` with the finding named — the SES-196 "named deviation, never buried" convention, and the same close the runbook already uses for a blocked build.
