# SES-132 — answered questions and decided cards take John's typed comments off the page with them

**Type:** Tooling · `P10 - Tooling` · tier `now`. Filed 2026-08-23 by runner cycle `2e8b0fab`
(directive `bee71cf4`, paragraph 2). The built half of that directive was the §4a daily-output
card, `v7.0.168`.

## John's report, verbatim

> "I could swear i have wrote comments in the gated questions, and most are not showing. shouldn't
> the thread show each page refresh and what your answers are?"

He is right. Diagnosis delegated to a Fable 5 subagent (register B21 — root-cause diagnosis is on
the delegate-to-Fable list) and returned with `file:line` evidence, verified against the live
tables rather than against what the docs claim the code does.

## Root cause

`thread(targetId)` is called from **exactly two places**: `docs/runbooks/briefing-template.html:501`
inside `card()`, and `:554` inside `question()` — and `visionClaim()` (`:567-575`) delegates to
`question()`, so §12 claims do render threads. `state.asks` is read nowhere else for display. **A
thread therefore renders only inside a still-live target, and there is no renderer at all for an
orphaned one.**

But the very act John performs on a target is what removes that target from the next rebuild:

| Section | Rebuilt from | What removes the target |
|---|---|---|
| §§5/6 cards | `runner_items WHERE decision IS NULL` (register B18) | he decides the card |
| §9 questions | `runner_questions WHERE status='open'`, newest 5 | he answers the question |
| §12 vision claims | undecided claims only | he decides the claim |

So his comment, **and the runner's written reply to it**, disappear from the page at the moment he
acts — while both go on sitting in `public.runner_card_asks`, displayed nowhere.

## Measured, not reasoned

Of the seven ask targets live in `briefing-state` on the night this was filed:

- **Still render:** `item-chi84-gate` (CHI-84 `decision IS NULL`), `q-adhoc-morning-standing`
  (`status='open'`), `vision-thesis-C-thesis-30` and `vision-mission-C-mission-29` (both
  undecided — note it was `mission-6`, a different claim, that he answered).
- **Vanish on the next rebuild:** `q-briefing-dom-fixture`, `q-desktop-remainder-keeps-slot`,
  `q-ladder-executable` — all three answered `yes` by him between `00:33Z` and `00:35Z`.

**It has already happened at least once before tonight:** `q-ladder-streak-reset` holds an answered
`runner_card_asks` row and is absent from `briefing-state`'s `asks` entirely. "Most are not
showing" is exactly what this looks like from his side, because the ones that vanish are precisely
the ones he engaged with most.

## Proposed fix — smallest correct, not built

After the section builders run, collect the target ids that actually rendered, then emit a
default-closed **"Answered — your past questions"** block calling `thread()` for every remaining
key of `state.asks`, headed by the target id (the thread's own q/a text is self-describing).

Primarily a **template-render** change. The **rebuild contract** in `briefing-page.md` gains one
line requiring every ask to be carried forward in `briefing-state` on every republish — already
stated at `briefing-page.md:511-513` and already violated once, which is the second half of the
bug. Optionally the departed target's title travels with the thread for a friendlier header.
