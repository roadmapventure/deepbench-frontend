onto an agent with no capability it would read as a second verdict-writer, which is precisely the

agent" (pattern:90). The cost of the chosen form is honest: the architect also inherits the gate and
auto-done parts of the Verifier's bar. That is additive context, not an authority — it holds no
capability — and when `AGT-492` gives it one, the lens can be narrowed then with a measurement.
