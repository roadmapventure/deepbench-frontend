Approved scope, recorded 2026-10-05 in session `bench-update-1005`. Every line below is John's own
requirement or his direct yes to a walkthrough question; ruling `1f8d7302-9354-42f4-bf04-f99c5464c14a`

## John's ten requirements (verbatim)

1. When clicking on Bench - it should now start with a homepage. Keep the wording at the top. However, the full roster does not appear. Instead, we are going to start with a dashboard.
2. Keep the top blue bar underneath the page text, but remove "add a player" and "roadmap venture".
3. Have cards that provide next items to do for the user. These include - "View or train my roster", "Connect to AI", "Add a player", "Test AI".
4. On the left nav filters, move "All" above "Private".
5. Change "Private" to "Private Agents".
6. "View or train my roster" opens up to the private filter.
7. "Connect to AI" - takes the current popup ai connect page, and makes it its own page
8. "Add a player" goes to the current add a player page.
9. "Test AI" is its own page, that takes all the testing wording out of the current AI popup and places here. This is just an informative page on how to test AI. The testing part no longer belongs to the Connect instructions.
10. Above the "All" filter, place a faint line, and this is the breadcrumb of where the user is, they can quickly go back to bench home, same as if they click on bench - it just gives them a visual where they are.

## Approved answers from the walkthrough

**Bench home (`/bench`).** Masthead wording unchanged ("Your agent roster." and its paragraph), the
brass rule, the blue stats bar, then the four cards. No roster grid, no vacancy tile on home. No
filter is highlighted on home; the Private Agents filter is no longer pre-selected on open.

**Blue stats bar.** On home it totals the whole bench: every agent under All plus the viewer's
Private Agents. On the roster it totals the selected filter, as today. "+ Add a Player" and the
workspace tag ("Roadmap Venture") come off the bar in both places, desktop and mobile. The striped
"Your New Agent" vacancy tile stays at the end of the roster grid.

**The four cards.** Order as listed; two-by-two on desktop, one column on mobile; the existing cream
card style with brass corners; the whole card is clickable; icon in a brass-ringed circle at the top
of the card (the vacancy tile's "+" circle treatment); no numbers on the cards.

| Card title | Icon | Line underneath | Goes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| View or train my roster | 👥 | See your private agents, open their files and add to their training. | Roster on the Private Agents filter |
| Connect to AI | 🔌 | Use your agents inside Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. | Connect to AI page |
| Add a player | ＋ | Create a new agent and start building their expertise. | The current Add a Player page (`/bench/new`) |
| Test AI | 🧪 | Teach your agent, then see what your training adds. | Test AI page |

**Left nav, shared by all five Bench pages** (Bench home, roster, Connect to AI, Test AI, Add a
player). Top to bottom: the breadcrumb in faint text with a faint rule under it; All; Private Agents
(label renamed from "Private"); the existing groups and the Product Team entry, unchanged. On home
the breadcrumb reads "Bench Home". Elsewhere it reads "Bench Home › <where you are>" — the filter
label on the roster ("Bench Home › Private Agents"), "Connect to AI", "Test AI", "Add a player" — and
"Bench Home" is clickable and goes where clicking Bench in the main nav goes. Clicking a filter from
any of the five pages opens the roster on that filter. On mobile, where the nav is already a chip
row, the breadcrumb sits as a line above the chips. The Personnel File is unchanged and keeps its own
"← Agent Roster" link.

**Connect to AI page.** The popup's content becomes a page. A "Which agent?" picker at the top lists
the viewer's Private Agents and any teams they are on; once one is picked, the Claude / ChatGPT /
Grok steps fill in with that name and address exactly as the popup does today (quick-add link, open
links, copy boxes, the team block). Opened from the Bench home card: nothing picked yet. Opened from
a private agent's Personnel File button ("Connect <first name> to AI"): the same page with that
agent already picked. The popup is retired — one Connect surface. With no Private Agents: the page
says so and points to Add a player. The old `/connect` link keeps working and opens the page with
the agent it has always pointed at already picked.

**What leaves Connect.** "Teaching your agent" and "Comparison test" both move to Test AI (John:
"move test to its own. put teaching your agent on there too."). Connect keeps the per-tool steps,
including the "simply ask for your agent to answer a question" line, and ends with one line linking
to Test AI: "Connected? Next: Test AI →".

**Test AI page.** Informational only, no agent picker. Carries "Teaching your agent" and
"Comparison test" with their current wording unchanged.
