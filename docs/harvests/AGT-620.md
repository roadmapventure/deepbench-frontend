Against 1,627,509 and 104,171 today. Nothing persistent was created; no migration was applied.

The migration's own guard was dry-run on the unchanged database as a bare `do` block that only raises: `hits 2433058 ms 2919.355 gate_idx_named f` -- so the guard refuses (and rolls the indexes back) when the indexes do nothing. `explain ... into j` inside plpgsql works on this server.

`public.capture_migration_down()` handles `kind: index`: for an identity that does not exist yet it derives `drop index if exists <identity>;` and classifies `auto-downable`. Both indexes are new, so the down is capturable from the ledger -- the property the orchestrator asked for.

Lock note: plain `create index` (not CONCURRENTLY -- `apply_migration` runs in a transaction) takes a SHARE lock on `backlog_items` until commit. The build is over 1,581 rows (milliseconds); the guard then runs the queue once (about 1 s after the indexes exist). Writers (claims, status writes) wait about 2 s at most; authenticator's `lock_timeout` is 8 s.

## 4. Alternatives weighed

- **Ticket step 1 (block (e) reads `count(*) from prime_directive_queue() where lane in ('drain','selfbuild')`).** Not built (D1). After AGT-637 it saves ~0.25 s, needs a CREATE OR REPLACE of an 11,145-byte plpgsql body mirrored in `docs/design/agt-496-outcome-check.sql` and pinned by `tests/regression/agt-496-outcome-check.test.mjs`, and puts a second copy of the gate's lane filter in the audit function -- the "cheaper source that quietly disagrees" risk the ticket itself names. If the Development Manager wants the sentence built anyway it is a separate, function-replacing change.
- **Rewrite `effective_need_score()` / `pick_exclusions()` set-based.** Larger reach (the predicate every pick reads), harder undo, and unnecessary: the index removes the cost without changing a line of the predicate.
- **Raise a role timeout.** Refused, same call AGT-637 made (fix the cost, not the budget).
- **Assert under 3 s as anon** (the ticket's last clause). Impossible without granting anon EXECUTE on an audit function, which widens exposure (`.claude/rules/supabase-column-grants.md` addendum: functions must be closed to anon by name). D2: the bar stays 3,000 ms, measured as the service key.
- **A time assertion in the standing regression test.** Refused per pattern:162 and the AGT-637 precedent: it would grade the live board and could turn an unrelated ship red. The time bar is asserted once, at QA 1, and the buffer bar inside the migration.
