## What "blank" means here, structurally

The AGT-162 bundle handler (`api/_lib/handlers/agent-bundle.js`, commit `efccedcf`, on origin/dev at `9e2c9ddc`) reads the primitives directly: the `agents` row (id, name, role, specialty, bio), `agent_configs` by type (`role_prompt`, `guardrail`, `output_format`), `assemblePrompt()` over assigned capabilities, `knowledge_entries` (status active, oldest first) and Eleanor's catalog via the broker with `requestingAgentId = brittany`. Blank therefore means: zero `agent_configs`, zero `agent_capability_assignments` (hence zero Skills reach her — `skill_profiles` has no `agent_id` column; Skills attach only through assignments), `data_room_access = '[]'` so the broker answers `denied-no-access` and the bundle reports it honestly (§19c, pattern:34), and `knowledge_entries` empty until the tester writes there. The seed's DO block asserts all of that, and the test's (b) arm pins the seed file to a single `INSERT INTO public.agents` with a mutation control.

The `bio` is the one sentence of content she carries. It is identity (who she is, that she starts blank), not a role prompt and not John's corpus; the bundle ships it as `agent.bio`, which tells the calling model exactly what the comparison test is testing. Rule #1 (§19d/§19e) is honoured: the bio names no other `agents.id`, and the test's (c) arm checks that word-bounded against the live id list.
