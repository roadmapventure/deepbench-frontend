## 2. Two gaps, both slice 2's own (pattern:124)

**(a) A refused row has no exit.** `classifyBoard` re-files a carried row nightly "awaiting the Development Manager's ruling" (`:685`). AGT-169 (v7.0.664) wired the ruler, and its call (i) is explicit: *the ruling unit is the check, never the row* — a `not-a-defect` on `owner:unrevalidated-30d` would retire the whole check, never close one row. So a refusal is a dead end: the row stays `open`, `revalidated_at` null, counted by `board-stale`, subtracted from every future batch. At 12 of 25 the drain floors near 200 rows and the v7.0.643 close condition (`revalidation 0 left`) is unreachable by construction — the stall its own revisit trigger describes, but structural rather than a stalled judge.

## 5. Rejected

- **Re-judge carried rows.** Twelve rows already ruled with cited evidence; a second model call per row is waste (pattern:9). They replay once from their own `judge refused:` detail — the file's own marker (`ingestJudgment:987`), now a constant, so the parse is structural (pattern:99). A legacy carried row without the marker (none exist: 12 of 12 carry it) simply clears and re-enters the batch.
- **Keep filing ledger rows for the slug.** The ledger row was a stand-in for a ruling that now exists as a decision. Filing both would double-record and keep AGT-131 raising an `owner:unrevalidated-30d` gap nobody can rule per row.
- **Fold proposals under the night's `hygiene` decision.** Coarse reversal (see §3).
- **Auto-finalise `removal proposed` → `removed` after the window.** A John rule (pattern:166); not touched. The consequence — proposed rows accumulate on his page — is stated in the STOP LINE as a fact, not an ask.
- **A skill-row change to the judge's answer schema** (e.g. a `dead`/`superseded_by` field). `agent-row-gate` answers no-authority for a `discovered` ticket (v7.0.643 call v, AGT-169 §2.6); `apply:false` + `reason` is already the contract's own removal sentence (*"else apply:false naming the superseding ticket or the evidence it is dead"*).
- **Reverse `4fb7083a` / `e6ef36c6`.** `e6ef36c6` imaged 25 rows at 06:02Z, 13 of which `ea89655a` stamped at 06:12Z; `updated_at` is never bumped, so a Reverse would restore all 25 images and wipe correct stamps.
