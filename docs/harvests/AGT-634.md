## 3. Decisions I made (reversible by `git revert`)

1. **Subject/verb narrowing, not an id requirement.** The finding's proposal (fire only when another id or a remainder is named) turns every id-less declaration false; those are real remainders. Wrongly-delivered strands unbuilt work silently, wrongly-short costs a re-pick -- so the reader stays generous to declarations.
2. **"otherwise partial" keeps firing.** It is the else-arm of an "ONLY if", and step 1's argument (a branch is not a declaration) could be extended to it. I did not: it fires today, the ticket asks for it to be covered, and making it silent would be a behaviour change in the stranding direction that nothing measured supports.
3. **`status` counts only at the start of a sentence.** "Status partial -- remainder ..." declares; "the status partial is what a refused settle writes" describes. The sentence splitter already starts a new sentence there.
4. **Step 4 is untouched.** A describing sentence that also names the ship's own id ("a refused AGT-nnn settles partial") still fires. No such kickoff exists among the 95 that I identified as the defect, the ticket is about the id-less shape, and widening the change to step 4 would move 95 answers on no measurement.
5. **A new test file, not more arms in `ses-385b-settle-ship.test.mjs`.** Two files instead of one, but that file's group (F) stays the record of AGT-128's measurement and keeps passing unchanged (measured: 7/7 related tests pass with S1+S2 applied).
