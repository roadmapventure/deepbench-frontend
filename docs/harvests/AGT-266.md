## 4. The design call: retire the record, do not edit it

The ticket asks for a correction record so "the next render drops it". A correction alone does not drop anything — it is an instruction in the prompt, and the defective sentence keeps loading beside it as *Supporting proof*. That is the instruction-level guard `pattern:10` warns about, and `pattern:1` says eliminate rather than bound.

`readRecords()` filters `status=neq.retired` (`:424`), so setting that one row to `retired` removes the sentence from every future render **by construction** — deterministic, no model compliance involved (`pattern:9`).

Retire rather than rewrite the body, because a stored record is what the agent said on 2026-09-24; the record kinds doc in the prompt says an objection "is superseded, never duplicated", and editing a stored answer in place would forge it. Retiring is one `update`, reversible by the down in the kickoff's §4.

Alternatives ruled out:

- **Edit the sentence in `body`.** Narrower, but it rewrites an agent's recorded answer and leaves no trace that the claim was ever made.
- **Retire all three 2026-09-24 variants.** Wider than the defect; `d8168640` carries no misquote (`pattern:65`).
- **Spend a real re-render turn** (the runbook's five-step render → sub-agent → write). It costs a judgment-lane call, produces new records rather than repairing old ones, and its pass/fail is a model's behaviour. The deterministic `--render` assert proves the same thing for $0 and cannot pass by luck.
- **A code-level check that every cited repo figure matches its source.** That is a real, larger capability — a general mechanism where a data fix serves today (`pattern:8`, `pattern:65`, `pattern:120`). Worth its own ticket; not this one.

## 5. What this does not do

It does not stop a misquote of a source that is not a repo figure, and it does not verify any other cited figure in any other record. It is one capability's correction record, not a platform-wide citation check. The standing rule is carried in the same row so a future render cannot reintroduce the figure "under a different wording", which is the durable half of the ticket.
