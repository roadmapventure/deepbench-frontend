## Alternatives considered and rejected

- **Edit the eight guardrail rows now.** Rejected: measurement 3. It is a gated write; a build that did
  it anyway would be routing around John's reserved authority.
- **Ship clause B as a hard assertion over the live rows.** Rejected: it would be RED on delivery and
  stay red until John signs, and a ticket does not ship a red test. It also grades the live world rather
  than the change (pattern:162). The report-or-ask form is green today, goes green on wording with no
  further code, and cannot be satisfied by doing nothing.
- **A new `.claude/rules/findings-intake.md`.** Rejected: `scripts/audit-corpus.js:560` globs
  `.claude/rules` into the governed corpus, so a new file there invites corpus findings for no gain. The
  directive's one home is the check's exported constant — the precedent `scripts/agent-row-gate.js` set
  when it called itself "the callable form of the rule" (pattern:8, narrowest layer that works).
- **A `docs/runbooks/runner-cycle.md` step line.** Rejected on measurement: ~380,862 bytes against the
  381,000 ceiling pinned at `tests/regression/ses-336-runbook-orchestration.test.mjs:87`, and its sha256
  is pinned in `docs/runbooks/cycle-card.md` and asserted at `tests/regression/ses-377-cycle-card.test.mjs:210`.
  Any byte change reds two green tests until `render-cycle-card.js --write` re-renders, and that render
  plus the `dm-knowledge-cycle-card` agent-row re-pin is ONE ticket-named operation (AGT-112). A cycle
  earlier today reverted a clean 5-byte runbook edit for exactly this and settled `partial`. Excluded and
  named in the STOP LINE rather than handed to the Builder as an impossible doc task.
- **A `runner_card_asks` row of `target_kind='skill-edit'`.** Rejected in favour of `'item'`/`'AGT-160'`:
  the `skill-edit` rows key on a `runner_staff_findings` fingerprint (`scripts/staff-watch.js:652`), and
  this ask is about a ticket. SES-394's own row is the precedent for `item` + a backlog id.
