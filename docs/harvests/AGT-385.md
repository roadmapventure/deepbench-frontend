## Approved answers from the walkthrough

**Choose an agent.** Heading "Choose an agent" (was "Which agent?"). Radio rows become cards. Agent card: the agent's icon, first and last name, role/title, and the team name only when the agent is on a team (no label at all when not). The picked card gets a brass border. John asked "Anything else?" and approved: nothing else on the card. After the agent cards, one card per team in the same style: the team name, the word "Team" where the role goes, and "<N> agents" where the team line goes; picking it connects the whole team (the address and team connection today's picker already offers). If the team data carries no member count, the card leaves the count off. "Pick an agent to see the steps." is removed.

**Select your AI tool.** Nothing shows below the cards until one is picked. After a card is picked, a heading "Select your AI tool" appears in place of today's "Connect <first name> to your favorite AI tool", with the three tabs Claude / ChatGPT / Grok in today's tab look and underline. NO tab is pre-selected and nothing shows below the tabs until the user clicks one. After a tab is clicked, that tool's content appears beneath without the tool-name heading above it.

**Claude tab.**
1. a link (not a button): "1. Click to add <first name> to Claude" (John's typo "Caude" is fixed). For a picked team: "1. Click to add <team name> to Claude".
2. "2. Make sure the Connector window has the name and URL already filled in. Click Continue." (John's text with "the" and "has" added.)

**ChatGPT and Grok tabs.** Claude is the only tool with a one-click link; on ChatGPT and Grok the link only opens that tool's settings page and the steps are the only path, so they are not a fallback and are not in a drawer.
1. a link: "1. Click to open ChatGPT Plugins" / "1. Click to open Grok Connectors" (opens the same page as today's button).
2. "2. Follow these steps:" followed by that tool's current steps and the copy box holding the address, shown open.

**FAQ section.** A heading "FAQ". Every drawer is closed by default. Drawers, in this order:

1. **What to expect after connection** — Claude: "Once connected, your first Claude session will ask your permission. Simply click "Always Allow"." and "You can turn off your agent by the same path: + → Connectors → switch off." ChatGPT and Grok: the same first sentence with the tool's name, plus that tool's current turn-off instructions (today's step-3 text for each tool, unchanged).
2. **How do I talk to my agent?** — "In a Claude session, simply ask for your agent to answer a question." (tool name swapped for ChatGPT / Grok; today's step-2 sentence for each). When a team card is picked it adds: "With a team connection, name the agent you want in your question."
3. **How do I test my agent?** — the Comparison test, wording unchanged:
   "Comparison test" then
   1. Ask a question that only your agent knows. Your agent answers from what you taught them.
   2. Then turn off your agent's connector, start a new session and ask the session (the AI tool's generic model) the same question. The answer is the model's own general knowledge.
   3. The difference is what your training brought.
4. **I can't get the connection link to work** — Claude tab ONLY (ChatGPT and Grok have no manual drawer). Opens to "Once in a Claude session:" and the eight manual steps with the copy box (today's step-1 list for Claude, unchanged, including the team block that offers the whole-team address). It is the last drawer.

ChatGPT and Grok therefore get drawers 1, 2 and 3.

Next: Test AI →" link and `CONNECT_SHARED.nextTest`; the "Add <agent> to Claude" button label and its note paragraph; the "If the fields come up empty…" line; the tool-name `<h2>` above the instructions; the always-visible step-2, team-step and step-3 paragraphs (their content moves into the drawers above). ## Build notes (named by the kickoff's T2/T3; style only, no copy)

- **B1 — QuickAddLink as a text link.** `<a href target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" style={{ fontFamily: body, fontSize: 14, fontWeight: 600, color: T.brassDeep, textDecoration: "none" }}>{label}</a>` — the same look as `ConnectAiScreen.jsx`'s `LINK` ("Add a player →"). - **B2 — Drawer.** `<details style={{ borderTop: \`1px solid ${T.line}\`, padding: "10px 0" }}><summary style={{ ...pStyle, fontWeight: 600, cursor: "pointer", margin: 0 }}>{title}</summary><div style={{ paddingTop: 8 }}>{children}</div></details>`. Native `<details>`: closed by default with no state, no `open` attribute anywhere.
- **B3 — Agent / team card.** `CARD = { display: "flex", gap: 12, alignItems: "center", background: T.card, padding: "12px 14px", marginBottom: 8, cursor: "pointer", maxWidth: 420 }`; picked: `border: \`1px solid ${T.brass}\`, boxShadow: \`0 0 0 1.5px ${T.brass}\`` (RosterScreen's AgentCard ring trick, so the layout does not shift); not picked: `border: \`1px solid ${T.line}\`, boxShadow: "none"`. Name: `{ fontFamily: display, fontSize: 16, fontWeight: 600, color: T.navy, lineHeight: 1.1 }`. Role line (and the word `Team`): `{ fontFamily: body, fontSize: 11, color: T.mutedDeep, fontStyle: "italic", marginTop: 2 }` (RosterScreen's role style). Team line (and `<N> agents`): `{ fontFamily: body, fontSize: 11, color: T.mutedDeep, marginTop: 2 }`, rendered only when there is text. Portrait: `<AgentAvatar who={a.id} size={48}/>`; team card `who={t.address}` (no `AVATAR_CFG` entry → neutral portrait). `onMouseEnter/Leave` hover to `T.brass` as the roster card does.
