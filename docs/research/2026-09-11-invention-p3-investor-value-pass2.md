## The pitch

Today the ledger's dollars are the platform's own arithmetic, and nothing outside the platform has ever confirmed them. `LOG-149` (committed today) freezes a price on every row from a JavaScript price book; the IP gate (§19t) blocks real visitors on a dollar figure computed from a second, SQL copy of the same book; the AI Audit's Total Cost tile and the About screen's "Every call is on the record" numbers are both sums of those computed prices. Anthropic bills from a different ledger — its own — and its cost report has never been read: no file in `api/`, `src/`, `lib/`, `scripts/` or `docs/` mentions the Admin API, `cost_report`, or an admin key.

The proposal is **Ledger reconciled to the provider's bill.** One runner step, every cycle, calls Anthropic's `GET /v1/organizations/cost_report` for the last two days grouped by `description` (which parses out `model`), and writes one row per day per model into `provider_cost_daily`. One SQL view, `spend_reconciliation`, joins that to the ledger's `cost_usd` summed by day and model, and returns ledger dollars, billed dollars, the delta, and the count of rows the ledger could not price. One line on the AI Audit beside Total Cost reads "Reconciled to Anthropic: $X billed vs $Y ledger, <window>, delta Z%", or "Not reconciled — no provider report" when the credential is absent (an em dash, never a fabricated match). No model call anywhere in it: the value is known deterministically, so a model may not be put in charge of it (`VC-INVAR-005`).

Why the system needs it, in order:

1. **It is the sentence in `VC-EXIT-021`, literally:** "costs that reconcile — because diligence trust dies on one wrong number." The acquirer's architect (`VC-EXIT-007`) does not want a cost tile; they want the tile's number tied to the invoice. This is the tie.
2. **It is the test for the two P3 proposals ahead of it.** `LOG-149`'s frozen prices and the still-unfiled Cost per deliverable both rest on the price book being right. The pricing page fetched today lists modifiers the book does not know — 1-hour cache writes at 2x (the log has a single `cache_creation_input_tokens` column and prices it flat at 1.25x), web search at $10 per 1,000 searches (the executor detects `server_tool_use` web_search blocks at `api/capabilities/execute.js:1287`; nothing prices them), US `inference_geo` at 1.1x. Reconciliation is how the platform finds out, daily, whether those gaps are dollars or rounding.
3. **It is the number John already governs on.** `ip_spend_report` shows his own row at $11.78 of a $20 cap with 3 blocked attempts, and a real visitor at $0.24 of a $5 cap. The platform is enforcing spend walls on a figure nobody has checked against a bill. `VC-INVAR-020` says any number that leaves the platform ships verified against its source with its defense; the source of a dollar is the invoice.
4. **It is S-sized on mechanisms that exist.** The cycle already runs every 3 hours and already hosts deterministic check scripts; the ledger already carries the four token counts; the AI Audit already renders Total Cost from one hydrated row set. One table, one view, one line, one env var.

The honest risk: read as a finance chore it is an administrative expectation (pattern 137) and classifies P10. Two things keep it P3. First, the moat pass of 2026-09-11 counts "AI cost attribution / FinOps" among the skill sets an evaluator scores, and the market shows this reconciliation being sold as a separate developer tool (Datadog, Vantage, Grafana integrations on Anthropic's own docs page) rather than shown inside a product to its buyer — the honest-audit-surface whitespace (`C-MAP-18`). Second, what makes it investor value rather than admin value is where it lands: on the public audit surface the diligence engineer already reads, as a claim with its defense, not in a back office. The one open dependency: the Admin API "is unavailable for individual accounts" — if John's Console is not an Organization, the feature renders its honest absence and the row tells him what to create.
