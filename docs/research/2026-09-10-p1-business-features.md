## The pitch

Every 2025-2026 report I could fetch says the same thing in different words: adoption is broad (88% of organizations use AI somewhere, per Stanford's April 2026 index) and shallow (agent deployment "in the single digits in many areas"). The people being hired at director level and above are the ones who can close that gap, and the postings say how they are screened: use-case selection first (the single most in-demand capability across 12,400 AI product postings, June 2026), then a record of shipping workflow products in a vertical (Anthropic's Workflows PM: "proven track record building vertical-specific or workflow automation products"), then the ability to translate AI capability into enterprise value (Microsoft AI's Principal PM). Nobody is screened on whether they can describe an eval harness.

So the portfolio needs to show business jobs finished, with the trust layer visible underneath when someone looks. The 25 below are ranked by how much a hiring evaluator for those roles would credit having built each one. The top five are: a work order completed end to end by a team of agents with the human at the gates; solicitation intelligence for government buys; contract intelligence over the data room; conversational analytics with governed metrics; and a cited account brief. Together they cover the trends every analyst names (agentic workflows, document intelligence, data agents, research agents, customer and employee service) and they all ride paths DeepBench already runs.

## What the market says (live, dated)

- Agents are the platform shift, and the numbers are enterprise-scale. Gartner (as cited 2026-02-24) moves task-specific agents from 5% of enterprise apps in 2025 to 40% by end of 2026. Forrester (2025-11-05) defines a "digital employee" as one that "independently executes complex tasks or end-to-end processes" and predicts half of ERP vendors will ship autonomous governance modules. OpenAI's ChatGPT Work (2026-07-09) sells shared agents that "prepare reports, write and execute code, respond to messages, and follow team-defined processes" and keep working after you close the laptop.
- The value sits in named functions. McKinsey's State of AI 2025 (as summarized 2025-12-15) concentrates value in customer operations, marketing and sales, software engineering and R&D, and says winners "rebuild processes (e.g., sales playbooks, support runbooks)". Deloitte (2026; 3,235 leaders) expects agentic AI to hit customer support hardest, then supply chain, R&D, knowledge management and cybersecurity, with a financial firm capturing meeting actions and an airline agent rebooking flights; 74% hope for revenue growth and 20% get it. a16z (2025-06-10): over 90% of CIOs tested third-party customer-support apps; enterprise search, data analysis and knowledge management are the active internal deployments.
- What people actually run on the APIs. Anthropic's Economic Index (2026-01-15) finds 75% of first-party API use is automation and names cold sales emails, email reply drafting, invoice processing systems, email classification and calendar scheduling; the March 2026 report finds sales and outreach automation (lead qualification research, data enrichment, cold-email drafting) doubling in share. OpenAI's enterprise report (as reported 2025-12-11) lists in-app assistant and search, agentic workflows, customer support, and data extraction and analysis as the 5x-growing API uses.
- Document work is where agents are being bought. Contract review has moved to multi-step agents that extract, redline against a playbook and produce structured tables with citations (Harvey 2026-06-01; TermScout 2026-07-23: review cycles down up to 80%). 67% of IDP initiatives now evaluate agentic approaches, and buyers require confidence scoring with human review (2026-05-20). - Service is measured in resolution rate and cost per outcome. Gartner (2025-03-05): agentic AI resolves 80% of common service issues by 2029 with 30% lower cost. The 2026 benchmark (2026-05-25): 66% of service orgs run AI agents, median tier-1 deflection 41.2%, AI resolution $0.99-$2.00 versus $6-$12 human. Claude for Financial Services (2025-07-15) makes "every claim links directly to its original source" the promise and names investment memos and pitch decks as outputs. StateChat reached 62,000 users with 90% reporting time savings (2026-07-27). Agencies use AI to cut FOIA review sets from 70,000 to 2,100 records (2026-07-15).
