## The goals, in his priority order

### 1. Numbers he can compute himself, that do not change between askings

His opening complaint: *"I have seen you use tokens, sessions, credits, and cycles very liberally.
This makes it hard for me to compute."* He wants units defined once and figures **stored, not
re-derived**. He is not asking for cleverness. He is asking for the same answer twice.

Settled definitions:

- **Token** — the real unit, roughly 4 characters. Everything else is built on it.
- **Cycle** — one run of the runner loop, one row in `runner_cycles`. Made of tokens.
- **Session** — one continuous context; may hold one cycle or several chained. Made of cycles.
- **Credit** — an Anthropic *overage* currency at metered per-token rates, drawn on only once the
  Max plan's included allowance is exhausted. **Balance $0.00 and never drawn on** — every logged
  cycle shows `api_cost = $0.00`. Never present credits as the cost of normal operation.
- His plan is a **flat Max subscription** (~$213/mo on the invoices he showed). Ticket volume does
  not change the bill; it changes throughput against a rolling usage window.

### 2. A backlog that answers the same question the same way every time

**This is the live sore point.** He asked "what is needed for M5" six times and got six answers.
Some changed legitimately as work shipped; at least three were the assistant re-deciding a
definition it had authored itself. Root cause: **"required for M5" is not a stored field**, so it
gets re-judged on every asking. `M5-04` forbids exactly this and was violated all evening.

**Agreed fix, not yet built:** store the required set as a field (e.g. `milestone_required`), set at
the gate decision, so the question becomes a query and any drift is checkable rather than
conversational. He asked whether Fable would answer better. The honest answer given was no — the
answer is not written down, so any model re-derives it and any model drifts.

### 3. M5 completed efficiently, close to its predicted budget, without him in the loop

Original prediction ~31.9M tokens. He wants it finished near that, uninterrupted, and explicitly
does not want to be the thing it waits on.

### 4. Protect Selfbuild's original scope, but let good enhancements in through a narrow door

He is not against new work. He is against silent scope growth. His required format for any new
ticket: **reason, suggested milestone, and the guard if it falls outside original scope.**

### 5. Autonomy without him as a gate

Verbatim: *"I no longer want to work via cards or taps. And you are supposed to be more self
sufficient to update tickets accordingly without me."* Decisions get made and recorded with
reasoning, reversible — never queued for him. **The one exception he did not lift:** `dev` to `main`
still requires his sign-off (`B20`, `HR-MERGE`).

### 6. Minimal cost to keep the loop running

He believes the drain and routines waste tokens, and the measurement agreed: a cold-booted cycle
ships a ticket for ~2.28M tokens against ~809K chained, and 53 fires in one week burned 32.4M
shipping nothing. He wants low startup and low keep-alive cost — **not the loop removed. He has
never asked to retire the routine; that was wrongly claimed twice.**
